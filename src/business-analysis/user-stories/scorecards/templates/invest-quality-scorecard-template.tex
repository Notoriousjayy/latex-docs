\section{Assessment protocol}
Each dimension is rated 0–3; a 0 in value or testability warrants explicit refinement regardless of total. Scores are discussion signals rather than mechanical entry gates.

\section{Interpretation}
Total possible = 18. Suggested heuristic bands: 15–18 strong; 11–14 improvement needed; 0–10 revisit fundamentals. Any score of zero in a safety-relevant, authorization, value or testability dimension deserves explicit review even if the total is high. Record concrete evidence rather than merely a number.
