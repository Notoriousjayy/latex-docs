\section{Scenario and initial inputs}
An internal service desk serves occasional requesters, frequent operators, reviewers and auditors. Interview transcripts are unavailable; distinguish testable hypotheses from observed facts.

\section{Procedure}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item List five candidate roles and their most important user goals.
\item Merge at least one pair of overlapping roles with justification; preserve one important distinction.
\item Prepare a six-question unbiased interview guide and a short observation checklist.
\item Create two role cards and a provenance table distinguishing assumption, proxy claim, direct observation and approved decision.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Submission checklist}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Two differentiated role cards
\item At least six neutral discovery questions
\item Three hypotheses with validation plans
\end{itemize}
\section{Verification criteria}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Outputs describe user outcomes rather than only technical activity.
\item Evidence is sufficient for another practitioner to reproduce or challenge the work.
\item Security, negative behavior and assumptions are visible when relevant.
\item Explicit tradeoffs and decision rights appear in the results.
\end{itemize}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=amberback,colframe=navy,title={Instructor note / expected reasoning},breakable]
Incorrectly treating the reviewer’s managerial authority as evidence of how requesters actually use the system.

\end{tcolorbox}
\section{Stretch exercise}
Exchange artifacts with another participant. Identify one unverifiable expectation, one dependency hidden by the initial plan, and one incomplete traceability link. Revise the record and document what changed.
