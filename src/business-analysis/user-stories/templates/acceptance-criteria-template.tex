\section{Use and completion rules}
Specify behavior with objective preconditions, actions and outcomes.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Work collaboratively with users, product decision-makers, developers and testers as appropriate.
\item Fill only the details justified by the current planning horizon; record unresolved questions explicitly.
\item Do not copy guidance text into a production record; replace bracketed prompts with actual evidence.
\item Link to relevant goals, tests and permanent constraints where traceability is warranted.
\end{itemize}
\section{Working template}
\subsection{Story ID and business rule}
[Trace to the capability and rationale.]

\subsection{Scenario ID}
[Stable acceptance reference.]

\subsection{Given / initial state}
[Concrete preconditions, roles and data.]

\subsection{When / trigger}
[Observable user or system event.]

\subsection{Then / expected outcome}
[Business-relevant result, not implementation detail.]

\subsection{Negative and boundary cases}
[Important failures, invalid inputs and limits.]

\subsection{Authorization / privacy check}
[Permission-sensitive behavior where relevant.]

\subsection{Test level and automation}
[Manual, API, UI, integration or nonfunctional.]

\subsection{Evidence / result}
[Build, date, status and defect reference.]

\subsection{Acceptance owner}
[Person authorized to confirm intent.]

\section{Reviewer checks}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Is the intent understandable to a person who does not know the implementation?
\item Does the record identify accountable decisions and meaningful verification?
\item Are dependencies and open questions visible rather than silently assumed?
\end{itemize}
