\section{Use and completion rules}
Decompose a large user goal into useful end-to-end increments.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Work collaboratively with users, product decision-makers, developers and testers as appropriate.
\item Fill only the details justified by the current planning horizon; record unresolved questions explicitly.
\item Do not copy guidance text into a production record; replace bracketed prompts with actual evidence.
\item Link to relevant goals, tests and permanent constraints where traceability is warranted.
\end{itemize}
\section{Working template}
\subsection{Epic / goal}
[User-level outcome beyond one iteration.]

\subsection{Roles and critical journeys}
[People, main action, and resulting value.]

\subsection{Boundaries and assumptions}
[What is in/out of scope and what is still unknown.]

\subsection{First thin slice}
[Smallest independently demonstrable path.]

\subsection{Second thin slice}
[New rule, role or data variation with distinct value.]

\subsection{Additional slices}
[Optional enhancements or alternate paths.]

\subsection{Vertical integrity check}
[How each slice works across relevant layers.]

\subsection{Dependencies / enabling work}
[Risk reduction or non-story activities.]

\subsection{Acceptance evidence}
[How each slice can be demonstrated.]

\section{Reviewer checks}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item Is the intent understandable to a person who does not know the implementation?
\item Does the record identify accountable decisions and meaningful verification?
\item Are dependencies and open questions visible rather than silently assumed?
\end{itemize}
