\section{Audit controls}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Use only eligible real meeting data in restricted organizational records.
\item Cite the controlling governing provision for rights, notice, quorum and vote requirements.
\item Require an independent verification of counts, exact motion text and approvals.
\item Do not publish personal membership, ballot or disciplinary data in this public repository.
\end{enumerate}
