\section{Instructional contract}
Twelve sequenced modules cover all 63 sections of the uploaded 12th edition. A module is passed only when the learner can demonstrate the listed artifact and explain at least one exception or conflict; classroom time suggestions are not a substitute for active practice.

\section{Module 1: Foundation: authority and assembly}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 1--3\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} None

Classify assembly types and control documents

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Produce an authority map with a verified hierarchy and membership register

\textbf{Gate:} No statement of authority is unsupported.

\section{Module 2: Motion mechanics and main questions}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 4--7, 10\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Module 1

Establish the floor, pendency, classification and framing

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Chair a motion from introduction to announcement, including an exception

\textbf{Gate:} Use exact final motion wording and correct class.

\section{Module 3: Sessions and business scheduling}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 8--9, 18--22, 41\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--2

Plan meeting types, calls, continuity, and privileged interruptions

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Prepare notice, agenda and a continuation decision

\textbf{Gate:} Identify electronic-meeting authority before simulation.

\section{Module 4: Subsidiary motions and amendment}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 11--17\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--3

Manipulate pending business without losing order

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Complete a two-degree amendment tree and a second motion interruption

\textbf{Gate:} No out-of-order amendment is put to a final vote.

\section{Module 5: Incidental motions and procedural challenge}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 23--33\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--4

Respond to points, appeals, requests, nominations and voting-method issues

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Chair five procedural exceptions with a ruling register

\textbf{Gate:} Preserve appeal question and outcome.

\section{Module 6: Disposal, reconsideration and renewal}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 34--39\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--5

Restore, rescind, discharge, reconsider and screen repetitive motions

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Analyze a prior decision under notice, time and reversibility conditions

\textbf{Gate:} Reconsider mover and timeliness are checked.

\section{Module 7: Quorum, debate and voting}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 40, 42--45\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--6

Validate capacity, discussion, majority, two-thirds and methods

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Run a quorum-loss drill and counted ballot

\textbf{Gate:} Denominators and abstentions are explicit.

\section{Module 8: Nominations, offices and records}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 46--48\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--7

Run elections, officer transitions and minute approvals

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Complete election packet, action register and minutes approval

\textbf{Gate:} Minutes record actions, not a debate transcript.

\section{Module 9: Boards and committees}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 49--52\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--8

Delegate correctly and report recommendations

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Create committee charter, referral and board minutes

\textbf{Gate:} No committee exercises undelegated final authority.

\section{Module 10: Formation, bylaws and amendments}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 53--57\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--9

Establish societies and manage governing instruments

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Conduct a mock organizing meeting and bylaw amendment

\textbf{Gate:} Notice and effective date are controlled.

\section{Module 11: Conventions}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 58--60\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--10

Credential delegates, organize committees and vote

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Prepare a convention credentials and rules packet

\textbf{Gate:} Uncredentialed persons do not enter vote count.

\section{Module 12: Discipline and capstone assurance}
\textbf{Reading:} RONR (12th ed.) Sections 61--63\quad\textbf{Prerequisites:} Modules 1--11

Maintain orderly proceedings and fair process

\textbf{Performance evidence:} Run a simulated annual meeting with challenge and independent review

\textbf{Gate:} No disciplinary decision lacks procedural fairness.

\section{Capstone standard}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Plan a valid meeting using fictional documents and documented notice.
\item Process an ordinary main motion, amendment, point of order, appeal, and non-unanimous counted vote.
\item Record the results in action-only minutes and correct the minutes by proper procedure.
\item Complete an independent assurance review with every rule reference tied to a 12th-edition section.
\end{enumerate}
