\section{Trigger and accountability}
\textbf{Trigger:} When a disruption or allegation arises\quad\textbf{Responsible:} Chair / authorized disciplinary body

\textbf{Primary reading:} RONR (12th ed.) 61--63

\section{Operating steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Distinguish immediate meeting disorder from an external case.
\item Protect decorum while using the least applicable intervention.
\item Check governing authority and required investigation or trial process.
\item Maintain impartiality, confidentiality and response rights.
\item Record only authorized decisions and secure evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Quality gate}
The secretary or designated independent reviewer must be able to trace the action to a valid convening authority, applicable governing rule, eligible voters where relevant, the chosen disposition and an official record. A process pass does not establish statutory legality.

\section{Exception and escalation}
Serious sanctions, conflicts of interest or legal implications arise. Document the issue, stop any unauthorized substantive action and obtain qualified parliamentary or legal guidance as appropriate.

\section{Retention and audit evidence}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Approved meeting notice and governing authority where applicable.
\item Attendance and quorum evidence when a vote is involved.
\item Exact action text, vote basis and final disposition.
\item Corrections, chair rulings and approvals needed to reconstruct the transaction.
\end{itemize}
