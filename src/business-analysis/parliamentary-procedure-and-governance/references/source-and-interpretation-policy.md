# Source, provenance and interpretive limits

**Primary source:** *Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised*, 12th edition, Robert’s Rules Association, PublicAffairs, 2020 (user-supplied reference PDF). Use section/paragraph references such as `RONR (12th ed.) 44:1`, rather than PDF page numbers, because print and PDF pagination can differ. The library does not redistribute the book or reproduce large portions of it.

The 63 guide titles reflect the source's 63-section structure and 20 major parts. All exercises, organizational examples, maturity scores, procedures, diagrams of decision logic, and illustrative entries are independently authored supplements, not official interpretations, model bylaws adopted by any real society, licensed legal advice, or publisher answer keys.

For correct application, establish the organization's actual charter, bylaws, special rules, parliamentary authority, governing law, and meeting circumstances; consult the primary book's exact paragraphs. The 12th edition discusses electronic meetings in 9:30--36; governance of remote participation and public bodies also depends on current governing law and duly adopted authorization. The formal handling of a point of order, appeal, special order, executive session, committee power, amendment or disciplinary case may have exceptions this suite does not enumerate.

## Evidence separation

- `study-guides/`: guided original analysis keyed to source sections.
- `technical-reference/` and `decision-matrices/`: original operational syntheses; verify exceptions before acting.
- `laboratories/`: fictional skill demonstrations, not real precedent.
- `procedures/`: administrative controls, not approvals by a real organization.
- `forms/`, `workbooks/`, `scorecards/`: paired authoring aids. Templates contain guidance and imaginary sample data; Blanks contain labels, open writing space and unmarked checkboxes only.

**Privacy:** Never commit completed membership registers, secret ballots, discipline records or confidential minutes to this public repository. This suite includes no actual personally identifying case data.
