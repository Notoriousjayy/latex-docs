\section{Scope and interpretation}
This is an original operational reference based on RONR (12th ed.). It is subordinate to applicable law and the organization’s validly adopted governing documents. Rule citations identify the primary source; the authors of this suite have not issued official interpretations.

\section{Determine applicable authority}
Begin with applicable law and the organization’s corporate charter or comparable foundational instrument. Then inspect constitution or bylaws, any adopted special rules of order, the designated parliamentary authority, standing rules and valid custom. Not every body has every layer, and statute or charter can override the usual ordering.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 2:1--25; 56:1--68.

\section{Conflict-resolution algorithm}
Record each conflicting clause verbatim within the organization’s internal working records; identify adoption date and legal status; determine which higher authority controls; escalate uncertainty to qualified counsel or parliamentarian. A frequent custom is not permission to override a written governing rule.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Deliverable: conflict log containing source, rule, conflict, controlling provision, owner and resolution.

\section{Amendment governance}
Distinguish a procedural special rule of order from a general administrative standing rule. Check amendment conditions in the actual bylaws and rules; do not transplant the vote threshold of one class into another. Ensure provisions affecting members receive required previous notice.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Test case: a committee wants to alter meeting frequency, nomination rules and office eligibility in one vote. Classify each first.

\section{Organizational control}
Maintain a controlled authoritative copy, date-stamped amendment register, annual consistency review and explicit approval chain. Include version control without treating a Git commit as legal adoption.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Assurance evidence: signed adopted text, notice proof, meeting minutes, votes, effective-date note.

\section{Independent review checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Find the controlling adopted text and note its revision date.
\item Specify the decision, eligible decision maker, notice, quorum and vote basis.
\item Record evidence without fabricating approvals or preserving private member data in a public repository.
\item Classify unresolved issues as parliamentary, legal, governance policy or factual disputes.
\end{enumerate}
