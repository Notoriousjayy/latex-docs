\section{Scope and interpretation}
This is an original operational reference based on RONR (12th ed.). It is subordinate to applicable law and the organization’s validly adopted governing documents. Rule citations identify the primary source; the authors of this suite have not issued official interpretations.

\section{Meeting validity}
Identify whether regular, special, annual, adjourned, executive, public, or electronic meeting provisions apply. Record the call/notice requirement, scope of special-meeting business, authority to convene, and the time and location or platform.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 8--9 and 41.

\section{Electronic authorization}
Under the 12th edition’s model, electronic meetings of an organization or board require bylaws authorization; see 9:30--36 for conditions, including simultaneous aural participation. Legal rules, particular bylaws and changed technology may require separate review.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Design requirement: confirm authorization, credential eligible voters, preserve recognition and continuously test quorum.

\section{Session continuity}
A recess pauses a meeting; adjournment ends a meeting; an adjourned meeting may continue the same session as specified. An agenda does not by itself supersede adopted orders. Track unfinished business deliberately.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Failure injection: a 15-minute recess becomes an accidental premature close; reconstruct valid continuation.

\section{Notice evidence}
Retain text, recipients, delivery channel, send timestamp, statutory requirements where applicable and any objection or defect. Do not assume a delivery screenshot proves compliance in every jurisdiction.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Control: create a notice approval and exception log before any binding vote.

\section{Independent review checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Find the controlling adopted text and note its revision date.
\item Specify the decision, eligible decision maker, notice, quorum and vote basis.
\item Record evidence without fabricating approvals or preserving private member data in a public repository.
\item Classify unresolved issues as parliamentary, legal, governance policy or factual disputes.
\end{enumerate}
