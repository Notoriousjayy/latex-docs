\section{Scope and interpretation}
This is an original operational reference based on RONR (12th ed.). It is subordinate to applicable law and the organization’s validly adopted governing documents. Rule citations identify the primary source; the authors of this suite have not issued official interpretations.

\section{Jurisdiction boundary}
Board powers derive from bylaws and the organization’s authority structure. A committee is ordinarily subordinate and reports to the body that appointed it; the wording of the appointment controls delegated scope.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 49--52.

\section{Committee charge}
Specify purpose, deliverables, membership and chair appointment, duration, report deadline, resource permissions, decision scope and escalation. Assign responsibility for records, not authority the parent assembly has not conferred.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Lab: mark each sample board and committee act as recommend, decide, refer or prohibited.

\section{Reporting}
Receive information without automatically adopting recommendations. A committee recommendation normally requires assembly action. Special reporting rules apply to minority reports and committee of the whole.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Deliverable: two recommendation motions with disposition and owner.

\section{Decision rights}
Construct a RACI-like table only as an administrative aid; it does not itself grant new rights under bylaws. Cross-reference every delegation to adopted authority.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Exception: an ex-officio board member’s rights depend on the applicable rule, not a general-purpose template.

\section{Independent review checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Find the controlling adopted text and note its revision date.
\item Specify the decision, eligible decision maker, notice, quorum and vote basis.
\item Record evidence without fabricating approvals or preserving private member data in a public repository.
\item Classify unresolved issues as parliamentary, legal, governance policy or factual disputes.
\end{enumerate}
