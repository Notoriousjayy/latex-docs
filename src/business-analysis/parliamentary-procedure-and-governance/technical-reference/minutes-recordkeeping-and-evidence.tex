\section{Scope and interpretation}
This is an original operational reference based on RONR (12th ed.). It is subordinate to applicable law and the organization’s validly adopted governing documents. Rule citations identify the primary source; the authors of this suite have not issued official interpretations.

\section{What the minutes are}
Official minutes document what was done, not a narrative of individual speeches. They identify the assembly, meeting type and timing, attendance of officers, approval of prior minutes, main motions and their dispositions, and other prescribed procedural details.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 48:1--15.

\section{Motion record}
Preserve the exact wording of adopted or otherwise disposed main motions, the mover where required, and the disposition. A seconder is not ordinarily recorded unless ordered; recorded count and teller results follow the governing recordkeeping rule.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Counterexample: a 25-page debate transcript cannot substitute for approved minutes.

\section{Approval and correction}
Maintain draft, distributed, corrected and approved statuses. Keep amendment provenance rather than silently overwriting an approved record. Establish retention and restricted access where law, bylaws or executive session requires them.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Test: correct a misspelled motion in previously approved minutes and document how the correction was authorized.

\section{Evidence register}
Use an ID, originating meeting, vote basis, authority, attachment link, custodian, access class and review date. Store sensitive records outside public repositories; public templates must contain fictional data only.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Assurance: trace a disputed decision from notice to recorded final text.

\section{Independent review checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Find the controlling adopted text and note its revision date.
\item Specify the decision, eligible decision maker, notice, quorum and vote basis.
\item Record evidence without fabricating approvals or preserving private member data in a public repository.
\item Classify unresolved issues as parliamentary, legal, governance policy or factual disputes.
\end{enumerate}
