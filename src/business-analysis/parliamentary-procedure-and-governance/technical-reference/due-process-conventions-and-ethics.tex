\section{Scope and interpretation}
This is an original operational reference based on RONR (12th ed.). It is subordinate to applicable law and the organization’s validly adopted governing documents. Rule citations identify the primary source; the authors of this suite have not issued official interpretations.

\section{Convention legitimacy}
Validate credentials, alternates, committee reports, standing rules, program and authorized voting membership before consequential proceedings. Separate the powers of a parent society convention from one not sponsored by a permanent society.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 58--60.

\section{Meeting order and discipline}
Distinguish immediate disorder that requires an in-meeting response from allegations outside the meeting. The available remedies depend on governing rules, officer authority and procedural safeguards.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Source anchor: RONR 61--63.

\section{Fair process}
A serious disciplinary case calls for defined allegations, impartial roles, notice and opportunity to respond, a fair hearing, a proper vote and secure records, as applicable. Neither accusation nor investigation establishes culpability.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Deliverable: process map with independence checks and appeal or review routes if required.

\section{No automatic legal conclusion}
Membership removal, officer discipline, confidentiality and due process may also implicate nonprofit, employment, privacy or other law. This educational suite is not a legal opinion.

\textbf{Practical audit hook.} Safety gate: stop any realistic personal case and obtain qualified advice.

\section{Independent review checklist}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Find the controlling adopted text and note its revision date.
\item Specify the decision, eligible decision maker, notice, quorum and vote basis.
\item Record evidence without fabricating approvals or preserving private member data in a public repository.
\item Classify unresolved issues as parliamentary, legal, governance policy or factual disputes.
\end{enumerate}
