\section{Denominator selection}
Majority and two-thirds without qualification are calculated from votes cast by persons entitled to vote, excluding blanks and abstentions (RONR 44:1 and 44:3). Other bases may require members present or entire membership; eligibility and quorum must be established before deciding.

\section{Election logic}
For a standard election requiring a majority, do not declare the largest plurality sufficient without a duly adopted exception (RONR 44:11, 46). Ballot validity and eligibility rules are source- and organization-specific. The chair must announce the result and the minutes must document required tallies and reports.

\section{Tally audit}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=4pt]
\item Verify the voting body and quorum independently of number voting.
\item Document yes, no, blank, abstention, invalid and unresolved ballots without treating them as interchangeable.
\item Identify the applicable threshold and denominator before counting.
\item Calculate the minimum affirmative votes required, then test the actual number.
\item Record the full result, any order for a counted vote and any tellers report.
\end{enumerate}
