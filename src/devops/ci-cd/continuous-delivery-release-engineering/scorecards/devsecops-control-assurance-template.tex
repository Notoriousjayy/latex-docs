\section{Scoring policy}
Score each dimension 0 (not implemented), 1 (informal), 2 (partial), 3 (implemented with evidence), or 4 (implemented, monitored, and tested under failure). Maximum is 32. Score 24 or above is a preliminary target; any critical missing trust boundary or recovery safety control is an automatic fail irrespective of total.


\section{Example assessment}
\begin{itemize}
\item Artifact identity and source traceability score 3 with immutable digest checks and missing expiry policy.
\item Security boundary score 2 where scoped OIDC exists but a negative authorization test is absent.
\item Finding is assigned to an owner with evidence and closure deadline.
\end{itemize}
