\section*{Chapter Overview}
Circuit analysis starts with conservation laws, explicit models, and measured limits.
\begin{cornell}
\noterow{Core idea}{Choose the simplest circuit model that answers the design question, then test its assumptions against loading, tolerances, and measurement conditions.}
\noterow{Validation}{Check KCL, KVL, component ratings, limiting cases, and a plausible physical interpretation.}
\end{cornell}
\begin{summarybox}{Section summary}
Reliable electronics work makes the model, operating conditions, and verification evidence explicit.
\end{summarybox}
