\section*{Chapter Overview}
This chapter organizes the mathematical and computational ideas behind partial differential equations. The notes preserve the numbered section sequence while emphasizing method selection, explicit assumptions, numerical reliability, cost, and verification.

\section*{Learning Objectives}
Explain the governing problem class; compare Flux-Conservative Initial Value Problems, Diffusive Initial Value Problems, Initial Value Problems in Multidimensions, and Fourier and Cyclic Reduction Methods for Boundary Value Problems; design defensible stopping and verification checks; distinguish problem conditioning from algorithmic stability.

\section*{Prerequisites}
Multivariable calculus, linear algebra, ODE solvers, Fourier analysis, and boundary conditions.

\section*{Operational Workflow}
Classify the PDE; specify consistent initial and boundary data; choose conservative, finite-difference, finite-volume, multigrid, or spectral discretization; enforce stability; refine space and time; check invariants and convergence.

\section*{Worked example}
\begin{exambox}{Independent worked example}
For advection speed $a=2$, grid spacing $\Delta x=0.1$, and a scheme requiring $|a\Delta t/\Delta x|\le1$, choose $\Delta t\le0.05$.  A step of $0.04$ gives Courant number $0.8$.  Stability does not prove spatial resolution or accuracy; perform grid refinement and conservation checks.
\end{exambox}
