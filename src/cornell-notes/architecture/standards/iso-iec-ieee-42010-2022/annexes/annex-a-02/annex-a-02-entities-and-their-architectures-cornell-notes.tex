\item There are several key aspects to the definition of architecture (3.2) Here.
\item This definition is chosen to encompass a variety of previous uses of the term “architecture” by recognizing their underlying common themes.
\item Principal among these is the need to understand and control those elements of an entity of interest that contribute to its utility, cost, time and risk within its environment.
\item In some cases, the fundamental elements are physical or structural components of the entity and their relationships.
\end{itemize}}
