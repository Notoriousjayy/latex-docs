\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Although sometimes used synonymously, Here they refer to separate and distinct concepts.
\item It is a goal of This section to encompass existing AD practices by providing common terminology and concepts.
\item Many existing practices express architectures through collections of models.
\item Typically, these models are further organized into cohesive groups, called views.
\end{itemize}}

\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
The terms architecture view and architecture viewpoint are central to This section. A viewpoint frames concerns and governs views; a view addresses those concerns using the viewpoint’s conventions. This material is informative guidance and does not itself create a conformance requirement.
