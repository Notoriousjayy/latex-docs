\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose / key concept}{Stakeholder perspective and aspect are closely related and often confused.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item While perspective is the way one thinks about something, aspect can be used for capturing the relevant features of the entity of interest.
\item An aspect of properties or concepts associated with the entity is perceived when viewing it and thinking about it from a particular perspective.
\item When the perspective is shifted, often the properties or concepts are different.
\item Likewise, when the viewing aspect is changed then different properties or concepts about the entity can be discerned.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Distinction}{A perspective is a way of thinking; it is not the stakeholder itself and is distinct from an aspect of the entity’s character.}

\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
Stakeholder perspective and aspect are closely related and often confused. A perspective is a way of thinking; it is not the stakeholder itself and is distinct from an aspect of the entity’s character. This material is informative guidance and does not itself create a conformance requirement.
