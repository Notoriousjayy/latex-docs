\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose / key concept}{This section uses the term concern to mean any topic of interest pertaining to the entity being architected or to the architecture itself.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Stakeholders, including the architect, of that subject entity hold these concerns.
\item Some concerns drive or relate to the architecture and therefore This section requires their identification as a part of the AD.
\item Concerns range over a wide spectrum of interests (including technical, personal, developmental, technological, business, operational, organizational, political, economic, legal, regulatory, ecological, social influences).
\item The motivation for using this term comes from the phrase “separation of concerns” in software and systems engineering, coined by Edsger W.
\end{itemize}}
