\begin{CornellOverviewBox}{Overview}
{\Large\bfseries\textcolor{CNavy}{Section 6.5: Identification of aspects}}\\[3pt]
{\small\textbf{Classification:} Normative}\\
{\small\textbf{Learning objective:} Identify the mandatory, recommended, and permitted provisions governing identification of aspects.}
\end{CornellOverviewBox}
\vspace{3pt}

\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose}{Establishes how relevant aspects are identified and associated with applicable concerns.}
\CornellNoteRow{Requirement}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must identify aspects considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD.
\item Each identified aspect must be associated with the concerns that apply.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Example / application}{Aspects include, among others, structural, behavioural, functional, programmatic aspects.}
\CornellNoteRow{Interpretive note}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item No particular method is prescribed for: the granularity and dependencies of aspects; how aspects relate to each other; or how aspects relate to other statements about an entity such as stakeholder needs, entity goals, or entity requirements. These issues are subjects for specific ADs, ADFs, ADLs, architecting methods, or other practices. See Annex F for examples of ADFs that use particular aspects and stakeholder perspectives.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Conformance check}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must identify aspects considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD
\item Each identified aspect must be associated with the concerns that apply
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Active recall}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Which provisions in Identification of aspects are mandatory for conformance?
\end{itemize}}
\end{CornellNotesTable}


\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
Establishes how relevant aspects are identified and associated with applicable concerns. For conformance, its mandatory provisions must be satisfied, including: A conforming architecture description must identify aspects considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD; Each identified aspect must be associated with the concerns that apply.
\end{CornellSummaryBox}
