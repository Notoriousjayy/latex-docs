\begin{CornellOverviewBox}{Overview}
{\Large\bfseries\textcolor{CNavy}{Section 6.10.1: Decision recording}}\\[3pt]
{\small\textbf{Classification:} Normative}\\
{\small\textbf{Learning objective:} Identify the mandatory, recommended, and permitted provisions governing decision recording.}
\end{CornellOverviewBox}
\vspace{3pt}

\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose}{Requires essential architecture decisions to be recorded and recommends a disciplined strategy for retaining important decisions, alternatives, context, and consequences.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Relations among decisions can be captured via correspondences or by applying correspondence methods.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Requirement}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must record architecture decisions considered essential to the architecture of the entity of interest within the scope and intended purpose of the AD.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Recommendation}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item The AD should record alternative decisions considered and rejected and the rationale for those choices.
\item The organization and/or project should establish a decision recording and sharing strategy and criteria for selecting important decisions to record and support with rationale in the AD.
\item When recording decisions, the inclusion of the following information should be considered:
\item Recommended content: unique decision identification
\item Recommended content: clear statement of decision
\item Recommended content: identification of decision authority or owner
\item Recommended content: identification of constraints and assumptions that influence the decision
\item Recommended content: link decision to the concerns or aspects of an entity to which it pertains
\item Recommended content: link decision to AD elements affected by the decision
\item Recommended content: link to decision rationale
\item Recommended content: relationships to other decisions
\item Recommended content: consequences of the decision (relating to other decisions) are recorded
\item Recommended content: timestamps for when the decision occurred, when approved and when modified
\item Recommended content: source citations for additional information
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Example / application}{Examples of types of relationships are: constrains, influences, enables, triggers, forces, subsumes, refines, conflicts with, exposes, and is compatible with (See Reference and Reference ).}
\CornellNoteRow{Interpretive note}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item An AD expresses decisions about an architecture. Architecture rationale expresses why decisions have been chosen.
\item The lists of decisions are not intended to be exhaustive.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Conformance check}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must record architecture decisions considered essential to the architecture of the entity of interest within the scope and intended purpose of the AD
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Active recall}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Which provisions in Decision recording are mandatory for conformance?
\item Which provisions are recommendations or permissions rather than requirements?
\end{itemize}}
\end{CornellNotesTable}


\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
Requires essential architecture decisions to be recorded and recommends a disciplined strategy for retaining important decisions, alternatives, context, and consequences. For conformance, its mandatory provisions must be satisfied, including: A conforming architecture description must record architecture decisions considered essential to the architecture of the entity of interest within the scope and intended purpose of the AD. Recommended practice includes: The AD should record alternative decisions considered and rejected and the rationale for those choices.
\end{CornellSummaryBox}
