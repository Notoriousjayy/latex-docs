\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose / key concept}{Stakeholders are parties with direct or indirect interests in an entity.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Among the stakeholders are those parties that have influence or control over and those who are impacted by an entity.
\item A stakeholder’s interests are typically expressed as concerns about an entity of interest or the architecture of which they are aware.
\item Concerns are often the result of the stakeholder's perspective gained from domain knowledge, experience, training, responsibility and authority.
\item Concerns are matters of interest or importance to one or more stakeholders.
\end{itemize}}
