\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose / key concept}{Architecture considerations are factors taken into account when architecting.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Concerns (see 5.2.3), stakeholder perspectives (see 5.2.4) and aspects (see 5.2.5) are different considerations to consider while architecting.
\item There are other considerations that can arise due to the architecture practices in use.
\item Architecture considerations are useful when specifying architecture viewpoints and when constructing, interpreting, organizing or using architecture views (see 5.2.7).
\item Architecture considerations can group specifications of architecture viewpoints, e.g. with respect to stakeholder perspectives.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Example / application}{Considerations include: the ability of stakeholders to interpret the architecture description languages chosen to express stakeholder views, the degree of formality required for viewpoints and model kinds, the availability of supporting tools, standard practices used in the given industry domain, the availability of time and resources, the criticality of the depth of understanding that stakeholders need to achieve.}
\CornellNoteRow{Interpretive note}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Other considerations can relate to contexts, criteria, building blocks and domain vocabulary.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Related sections}{5.2.3, 5.2.4, 5.2.5, 5.2.7}

\end{CornellNotesTable}
