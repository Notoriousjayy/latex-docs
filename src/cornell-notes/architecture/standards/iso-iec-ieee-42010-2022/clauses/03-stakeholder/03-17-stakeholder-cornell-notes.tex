\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Term}{\textbf{stakeholder}}
\CornellNoteRow{Definition}{A role, position, person, organization, or class thereof having an interest, right, share, or claim in an entity of interest.}
\CornellNoteRow{Distinction}{A stakeholder is an interested party or role; a stakeholder perspective is a way of thinking about the entity that can shape concerns.}

\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
A role, position, person, organization, or class thereof having an interest, right, share, or claim in an entity of interest. A stakeholder is an interested party or role; a stakeholder perspective is a way of thinking about the entity that can shape concerns.
