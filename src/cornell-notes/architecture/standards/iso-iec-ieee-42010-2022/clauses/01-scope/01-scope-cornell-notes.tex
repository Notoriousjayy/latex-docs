{\small\textbf{Classification:} Scope}\\
{\small\textbf{Learning objective:} Recognize what is covered by the architecture-description requirements and what is explicitly outside their scope.}
\end{CornellOverviewBox}
\vspace{3pt}

\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose}{Defines the applicability and boundaries of architecture-description requirements, including the work products and supporting specifications that are covered and the processes, tools, formats, and entity requirements that are not prescribed.}
\CornellNoteRow{Core details}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item The scope establishes requirements for the structure and expression of an architecture description (AD) for various entities, including software, systems, enterprises, systems of systems, families of systems, products (goods or services), product lines, service lines, technologies and business domains.
\item A key boundary distinguishes the architecture of an entity of interest from an AD expressing that architecture.
\item Architectures are not the subject of This section.
\item The scope establishes requirements for use of the architectural concepts and their relationships as captured in an AD.
\end{itemize}}
