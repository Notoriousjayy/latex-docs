\begin{CornellOverviewBox}{Overview}
{\Large\bfseries\textcolor{CNavy}{Section 6.3: Identification of stakeholder perspectives}}\\[3pt]
{\small\textbf{Classification:} Normative}\\
{\small\textbf{Learning objective:} Identify the mandatory, recommended, and permitted provisions governing identification of stakeholder perspectives.}
\end{CornellOverviewBox}
\vspace{3pt}

\begin{CornellNotesTable}
\CornellNoteRow{Purpose}{Establishes how relevant stakeholder perspectives are identified, associated with stakeholders, and connected to resulting concerns.}
\CornellNoteRow{Requirement}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must identify stakeholder perspectives considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD.
\item An AD must associate each identified perspective with the identified stakeholders holding that perspective.
\item For Each identified perspective, an AD must enumerate its resulting concerns from among identified concerns (per 6.4).
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Recommendation}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Within the scope of the intended purpose of the AD, the architecting effort should identify present or future stakeholder perspectives which may be relevant to the entity of interest.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Example / application}{Stakeholder perspectives include: strategic, organizational, operational, logical, physical and technological perspectives.}
\CornellNoteRow{Interpretive note}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item No particular method is prescribed for: the granularity of concerns; the granularity and dependencies of stakeholder perspectives; how stakeholder perspectives relate to each other; or how stakeholder perspectives relate to other statements about an entity such as stakeholder needs, entity goals, or entity requirements. These issues are subjects for specific AD, ADFs, architecting methods, or other practices.
\item See Annex F for examples of stakeholder perspectives as used in ADFs.
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Related sections}{6.4}
\CornellNoteRow{Conformance check}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item A conforming architecture description must identify stakeholder perspectives considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD
\item An AD must associate each identified perspective with the identified stakeholders holding that perspective
\end{itemize}}
\CornellNoteRow{Active recall}{\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep,topsep=1pt]
\item Which provisions in Identification of stakeholder perspectives are mandatory for conformance?
\item Which provisions are recommendations or permissions rather than requirements?
\end{itemize}}
\end{CornellNotesTable}


\begin{CornellSummaryBox}{Summary}
Establishes how relevant stakeholder perspectives are identified, associated with stakeholders, and connected to resulting concerns. For conformance, its mandatory provisions must be satisfied, including: A conforming architecture description must identify stakeholder perspectives considered relevant to the architecture of the entity of interest and consistent with the purpose of the AD; An AD must associate each identified perspective with the identified stakeholders holding that perspective. Recommended practice includes: Within the scope of the intended purpose of the AD, the architecting effort should identify present or future stakeholder perspectives which may be relevant to the entity of interest.
\end{CornellSummaryBox}
