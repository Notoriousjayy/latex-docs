\section{System overview and drivers}
\subsection{Distinct profiles and models}
Practice is the default, optional live accounts change the trust boundary. Do not republish real mailbox content. System One supports classification, selection and evidence ranking; System Two reasons and proposes tools. Rule-based fallback is documented. Neither probabilistic model grants permission. Governed definitions and skill disclosure provide domain context.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-1, SAD-P02-EVD-5, SAD-P02-EVD-6, SAD-P02-EVD-7, SAD-P02-EVD-8, SAD-P02-EVD-21.\par

\subsection{Bounded control loop}
The graph validates typed proposals, checks model-facing access, calculates effect tier and refusal, and bounds tool iterations. Gated actions are drafted and persisted before interrupt. After resume the audit row, not the resume payload, controls execution. APPROVED calls revalidate arguments; DRAFTED is rejected if no decision exists. An effect precedes recording its outcome, so crash windows need replay testing. Process-local thread locks are not a distributed exactly-once guarantee.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-2, SAD-P02-EVD-3, SAD-P02-EVD-4, SAD-P02-EVD-23.\par

\subsection{Workspace and trust boundary}
MCP mail, calendar and notes capabilities do not imply three deployed servers. The gateway, provider scopes, safe-mode decision and application policy must be assessed independently. Inbox rendering and heuristic screening reduce exposure but cannot prove resistance to injection. Test direct gateway calls and unauthorized external effects.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-6, SAD-P02-EVD-7, SAD-P02-EVD-8, SAD-P02-EVD-9, SAD-P02-EVD-10.\par

\subsection{Time and information}
Scheduler rearm, due-job handlers, focus timers and routines persist work across requests. Task source linkage supports deduplication review. Memory expiry, deletion and scratch promotion separate transient and durable state. Deployment topology, principal isolation, retention and recovery remain unverified.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-11, SAD-P02-EVD-12, SAD-P02-EVD-13, SAD-P02-EVD-14, SAD-P02-EVD-15, SAD-P02-EVD-16, SAD-P02-EVD-17, SAD-P02-EVD-18, SAD-P02-EVD-19, SAD-P02-EVD-20, SAD-P02-EVD-22.\par

\subsection{Packaged and research variants}
MemoRizz, pi, Hermes and DeepSeek are documented alternatives; do not inherit custom authorization semantics. Research uses separate outline/publication gates and fan-out writing. URL canonicalization and stored sources improve provenance, not citation truth. Notebook-only integrations and actual provider behavior remain partial.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-24, SAD-P02-EVD-25, SAD-P02-EVD-26, SAD-P02-EVD-27, SAD-P02-EVD-28, SAD-P02-EVD-29.\par

\subsection{Evolution from ERPA}
Part 1 curated evidence and mostly internal operations contrast with untrusted third-party content, outward effects and proactive timing. Proposed production controls are scoped identity, deterministic policy enforcement, idempotent delivery and missing-evidence denial.\par\textbf{Evidence:} SAD-P02-EVD-1, SAD-P02-EVD-2, SAD-P02-EVD-4, SAD-P02-EVD-8, SAD-P02-EVD-14.\par
