## Attribution and interpretation policy

- **Book-grounded scope:** chapter names, domain themes, general reuse/adaptation intent, and printed chapter references.
- **Original synthesis:** educational prose, exercises, model review questions and illustrative conceptual patterns.
- **Supplementary modern engineering:** relational normalization exercises, PostgreSQL DDL, UML/PlantUML examples, schema-change automation, governance operations, security controls, analytics, and review scorecards. None is presented as content from the original publication.
- **No reproduced proprietary diagrams:** all `.puml` and SQL examples are independently composed. No book images, pages, tables or bundled CD-ROM content are included in the repository package.
- **Reference models are not schemas:** a domain reference should be adapted with bounded-context ownership, business validation, and an approved migration plan.
