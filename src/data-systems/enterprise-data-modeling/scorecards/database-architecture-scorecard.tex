\section{Scoring protocol}
Score each criterion 0--4: 0 absent, 1 unverified assertion, 2 partial evidence, 3 reviewed and tested, 4 continuously verified with ownership and records. For each row, normalized contribution equals Weight × Score/4. Do not substitute a positive average for a failed critical integrity or security control. Record unavailable evidence as zero unless the review formally approves a not-applicable exclusion and redistributes weights proportionately.

\section{Interpretation}
Readiness targets: 90--100 Strong; 80--89 Reviewable; 65--79 Remediation Required; below 65 Not Ready. Any unresolved critical security or integrity issue blocks production readiness regardless of numeric score. The score is a decision aid and must never be presented as formal certification.


\section{Approval and follow-up}
Record assessed version, owner, reviewers, evidence as-of date, scope, findings, assigned remediation, re-review target and final decision. Use the separate printable Scorecard Template and Blank forms for controlled reviews.
