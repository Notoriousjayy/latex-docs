\section{Forecast logic}
Illustration assumes all revenue is collected and operating bills paid within the same month, no taxes, finance or depreciation, and opening cash of 10000. A realistic model adds receivables, payables, capital spending, taxes and financing and reconciles profit to cash.\par\textbf{Educational assumption:} all figures are fictional; collection, expenses, taxes and financing must be adapted and reconciled before use.

\section{Assurance tests}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Verify each opening-to-closing balance and cost summation.
\item Refresh with actual collections or accruals and explain variance by driver.
\item Document the first breached operating or liquidity threshold.
\item Attach scenario owners and an accountable reviewer.
\end{enumerate}
