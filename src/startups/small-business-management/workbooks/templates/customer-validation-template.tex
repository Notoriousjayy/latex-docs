\section{Instructions}
This workbook models a repeatable workflow. The row below is deliberately fictional and may contain rounded values. Validate formulas, denominators and units rather than copying sample numbers into business decisions.
\noindent\textbf{Hypothesis ID:} H-01\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Interviewee segment:} Home services\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Observed current approach:} Spreadsheet reminders\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Cost or frequency:} 3 missed leads/week\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Purchase or behavior signal:} Accepted paid pilot\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Contradiction:} Some prefer phone\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Experiment result:} 2 of 5 pilot commitments\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Decision:} Revise target segment\par\medskip
\section{Verification steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item List primary input sources and the date each was observed.
\item Recalculate any derived figures, identifying denominator and rounding choice.
\item Compare a base case with a downside scenario and one alternative.
\item Record what would invalidate the conclusion and the next test.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Workbook reflection}
Which input is least reliable? Which decision would change if it moved by 20 percent? Document the answer with evidence and reviewer sign-off.
