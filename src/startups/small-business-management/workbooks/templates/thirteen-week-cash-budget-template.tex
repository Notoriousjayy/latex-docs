\section{Forecast logic}
Closing cash = opening cash + receipts minus operating payments, debt service and capital spending. The first four rows illustrate a 12000 cash floor. Roll closing to next opening; the remaining weeks are for practice.\par\textbf{Educational assumption:} all figures are fictional; collection, expenses, taxes and financing must be adapted and reconciled before use.

\section{Assurance tests}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Verify each opening-to-closing balance and cost summation.
\item Refresh with actual collections or accruals and explain variance by driver.
\item Document the first breached operating or liquidity threshold.
\item Attach scenario owners and an accountable reviewer.
\end{enumerate}
