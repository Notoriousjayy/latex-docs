\section{Rubric and evidence interpretation}
Scores: 0 missing; 1 assertion only; 2 partial or unverified evidence; 3 relevant dated evidence with reasoning; 4 independent corroboration and replicable checks. Evidence must be examined before rating; a high average does not override a critical missing control.
\subsection*{Source quality}
\textbf{Inspection criterion:} Actuals and assumptions distinguished.\par\textit{Illustrative rating logic:} award 1 for a claim alone, 2 for a documented but untested approach, 3 for executed evidence, and 4 when an independent checker reproduces the conclusion.\par

\section{Gate decision}
Calculate the mean across six equally weighted criteria. Instructional passing example: a mean of 3 or better with every critical safety, ethics, legality or cash-solvency item at least 2. Record any exception, owner, mitigation and next assessment.
