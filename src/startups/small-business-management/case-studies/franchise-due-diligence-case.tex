\section{Situation}
An aspiring operator compares buying a franchise with launching an independent food-service concept. The franchise promotes brand recognition but restricts vendors and imposes continuing fees.
\section{Case evidence (fictional)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Initial franchise fee 30000; setup and equipment another 120000
\item Proposed royalty 6 percent of gross sales; marketing fee 2 percent
\item Independent concept setup estimate 100000, but demand is less certain
\item Neither option has verified local demand, net margins or a final lease
\end{itemize}

\section{Student tasks}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Classify irrecoverable costs, royalties and dependencies.
\item Prepare a diligence request list that includes operating-unit economics and contract exit terms.
\item Show a royalty sensitivity table at three revenue levels.
\item State which missing data makes a decision premature.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Required evidence packet}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Decision statement with clearly stated constraints and alternatives.
\item Calculation sheet where numerical claims are made; show units and sensitivity.
\item Risk register with owner, response and residual uncertainty.
\item Recommendation, go/no-go threshold and 30-day follow-up plan.
\end{itemize}

\section{Instructor review notes}
A successful answer does not depend on the same choice as an instructor example. Judge the quality of evidence, arithmetic, critical assumptions, operational feasibility and treatment of adverse effects. Withhold approval when missing financial or compliance information could reverse the recommendation.
