\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Customer problem}
\textbf{Method:} Identify a costly recurring problem and named affected parties.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Observed manual handoffs delay weekly reconciliation for boutique agencies.\par
\subsection*{Target buyer and user}
\textbf{Method:} Distinguish payer from beneficiary.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Agency owner buys; operations coordinator uses.\par
\subsection*{Existing alternatives}
\textbf{Method:} Include no-purchase and manual substitutes.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Spreadsheet plus two contractor tools.\par
\subsection*{Evidence sources}
\textbf{Method:} Record independent source, date and limitations.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Five interviews, dated observations, two test offers.\par
\subsection*{Economic hypothesis}
\textbf{Method:} State unit, currency, acquisition and support costs.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Monthly service fee against time saved.\par
\subsection*{Kill criteria}
\textbf{Method:} Define a falsifiable, time-bound rejection rule.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Stop if pilot users do not return in week two.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
