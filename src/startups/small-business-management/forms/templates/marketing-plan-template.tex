\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Segment and pain}
\textbf{Method:} Specify a primary customer and urgent trigger.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Service firms losing leads after business hours.\par
\subsection*{Positioning and promise}
\textbf{Method:} Avoid unverifiable guarantees.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Same-day follow-up workflow with audit trail.\par
\subsection*{Channel and spend allocation}
\textbf{Method:} Define channel economics and budget basis.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Referral 50\%; content 30\%; outreach 20\%.\par
\subsection*{Funnel assumptions}
\textbf{Method:} Log denominator and calendar period.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} 100 leads, 20 demos, 6 paid conversions.\par
\subsection*{Retention and service}
\textbf{Method:} Plan both acquisition and repeat value.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Monthly check-in and first-week onboarding.\par
\subsection*{Measurements and review}
\textbf{Method:} Tie thresholds to decisions.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Acquisition cost, paid activation, churn, gross contribution.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
