\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Funding objective}
\textbf{Method:} Specify purpose and timeline.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Fund six months of product validation.\par
\subsection*{Use of proceeds}
\textbf{Method:} Distinguish operating reserve from investment.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Build 18,000; sales 6,000; reserve 12,000.\par
\subsection*{Sources and availability}
\textbf{Method:} Validate each conditional source.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Founder equity 20,000; proposed loan 16,000.\par
\subsection*{Debt service and terms}
\textbf{Method:} Include maturity, rate, collateral and covenants.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Proposed monthly payment; underwriting pending.\par
\subsection*{Ownership and control}
\textbf{Method:} Record dilution and voting implications.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} No new equity proposed.\par
\subsection*{Contingency and go/no-go}
\textbf{Method:} Tie funding dependency to cash threshold.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Reduce hiring if funding is delayed.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
