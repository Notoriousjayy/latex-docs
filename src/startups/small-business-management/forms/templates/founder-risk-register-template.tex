\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Risk event and cause}
\textbf{Method:} Use event/cause/consequence structure.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Single vendor blocks customer onboarding.\par
\subsection*{Exposure and evidence}
\textbf{Method:} Record rating rationale and uncertainty.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Medium likelihood; high service impact.\par
\subsection*{Treatment strategy}
\textbf{Method:} Choose avoid/reduce/transfer/accept.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Secondary vendor and manual fallback.\par
\subsection*{Control owner and due date}
\textbf{Method:} Ensure decision authority.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Operations lead; next quarterly review.\par
\subsection*{Residual risk and trigger}
\textbf{Method:} Do not claim success without test evidence.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Elevated until failover drill succeeds.\par
\subsection*{Monitoring and escalation}
\textbf{Method:} Specify visible triggers and response.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Alert when integration failure exceeds threshold.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
