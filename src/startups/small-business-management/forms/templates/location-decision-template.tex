\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Operating formats}
\textbf{Method:} Include online footprint where applicable.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Home office, shared workspace, light retail site.\par
\subsection*{Customer and supplier access}
\textbf{Method:} Quantify access importance.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Three clients require on-site visits monthly.\par
\subsection*{Fixed and moving costs}
\textbf{Method:} Use full annual costs and one-time commitments.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Base rent, internet, setup, commuting and insurance.\par
\subsection*{Legal and operational constraints}
\textbf{Method:} Seek jurisdiction-specific verification.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Zoning and occupancy questions unresolved.\par
\subsection*{Weighted alternatives}
\textbf{Method:} Document weights before scoring.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Score access, cost, flexibility and risk.\par
\subsection*{Decision and fallback}
\textbf{Method:} Preserve reversible choice and contingency.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Select home office pending permit review.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
