\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Founders and ownership}
\textbf{Method:} Differentiate current agreement from proposal.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Two founders with proposed unequal equity.\par
\subsection*{Role and decision rights}
\textbf{Method:} Record escalation and tie-breaker.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Product lead and operating lead; written spending limits.\par
\subsection*{Entity options}
\textbf{Method:} Capture advice requested, not generic legal conclusions.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} LLC and corporation evaluated.\par
\subsection*{Liability, taxes and transfer}
\textbf{Method:} Identify unresolved questions for advisors.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Professional review planned for state-specific differences.\par
\subsection*{Alliances and contractors}
\textbf{Method:} Check control, confidentiality and classification.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Design agency under scoped statement of work.\par
\subsection*{Approvals and next review}
\textbf{Method:} Sign and date decision once authorized.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Founders to meet counsel before filing.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
