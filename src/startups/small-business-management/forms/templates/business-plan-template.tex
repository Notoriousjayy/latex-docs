\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Executive summary}
\textbf{Method:} Include funding need and evidence strength.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Niche scheduling workflow; 90-day validation phase.\par
\subsection*{Company and product}
\textbf{Method:} Explain delivery and differentiated value.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Subscription application for service providers.\par
\subsection*{Market and competitors}
\textbf{Method:} Use attainable customer counts and primary observations.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Regional microbusiness segment; two incumbent alternatives.\par
\subsection*{Marketing and sales}
\textbf{Method:} Define funnel and sales capacity.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Referral plus product demo; trial-to-paid metric.\par
\subsection*{Operations, organization and location}
\textbf{Method:} Document ownership and service continuity.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Remote delivery, contractor support, hosting controls.\par
\subsection*{Financial plan, risks and exit}
\textbf{Method:} Reconcile scenarios and key assumptions.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Monthly P\&L, cash forecast, funding gap, transfer readiness.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
