\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Owner objectives}
\textbf{Method:} State financial and nonfinancial priorities.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Preserve customer service through eventual sale.\par
\subsection*{Potential exit routes}
\textbf{Method:} Compare control, complexity and time.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Management buyout or strategic acquirer.\par
\subsection*{Transferability}
\textbf{Method:} Identify buyer-readiness evidence.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Written contracts, clean IP rights, documented processes.\par
\subsection*{Valuation assumptions}
\textbf{Method:} Avoid guaranteed valuation claims.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Earnings normalization and customer concentration review.\par
\subsection*{Succession and continuity}
\textbf{Method:} Record owner dependencies.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Deputy manager trained for core operations.\par
\subsection*{Decision triggers and advisers}
\textbf{Method:} Seek tax/legal valuation advice.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Review on profitability and founder availability.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
