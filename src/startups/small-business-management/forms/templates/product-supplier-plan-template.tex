\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Customer requirement}
\textbf{Method:} Connect to service quality and demand.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Two-day turnaround on standard request.\par
\subsection*{Process and capacity}
\textbf{Method:} Account for variation and rework.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Three requests per specialist per day.\par
\subsection*{Supply dependencies}
\textbf{Method:} Map contracts and substitutes.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} One print supplier, two packaging suppliers.\par
\subsection*{Quality acceptance}
\textbf{Method:} Define measurement and inspection.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} First-pass defect target below 2\%.\par
\subsection*{Inventory and reorder}
\textbf{Method:} Model stockout and cash costs.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Buffer based on demand and lead time.\par
\subsection*{Contingency and owner}
\textbf{Method:} Drill fallback before dependence grows.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Use alternate supplier with preapproved sample.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
