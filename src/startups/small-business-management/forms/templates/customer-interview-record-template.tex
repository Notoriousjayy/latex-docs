\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Interview date and segment}
\textbf{Method:} Record date and role only when lawful and needed.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Operations manager at 10-person service firm.\par
\subsection*{Observed job-to-be-done}
\textbf{Method:} Capture actual tasks and workarounds.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Consolidate appointments, invoices and follow-up.\par
\subsection*{Current effort and cost}
\textbf{Method:} Distinguish estimate from measured activity.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Three hours per weekly batch.\par
\subsection*{Evidence of urgency}
\textbf{Method:} Record behavior rather than compliments.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Customer paid for manual assistant.\par
\subsection*{Purchase authority and constraints}
\textbf{Method:} Clarify buying process and dependencies.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Owner controls software budget.\par
\subsection*{Next test and owner}
\textbf{Method:} Define follow-up evidence and decision deadline.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Two-week paid concierge pilot.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
