\section{Use and decision rule}
Use this guided Template to understand how to complete the companion Blank. Fictional examples are illustrative only. Mark each assertion as observed, estimated or proposed; identify a review owner before operational use.
\subsection*{Customer segment}
\textbf{Method:} Specify one reachable beachhead.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Independent field-service businesses.\par
\subsection*{Value proposition}
\textbf{Method:} Make benefit observable.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Fewer missed service appointments.\par
\subsection*{Channels and relationships}
\textbf{Method:} Separate acquisition from ongoing support.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Referral partnerships and assisted onboarding.\par
\subsection*{Revenue mechanics}
\textbf{Method:} List billing triggers, payment lag and refunds.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Per-active-team subscription.\par
\subsection*{Cost and delivery mechanics}
\textbf{Method:} Split variable, fixed and step costs.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Hosting, support, payment fees and labor.\par
\subsection*{Key partners, risks and tests}
\textbf{Method:} Link partners to mitigation and test.\par\textbf{Fictional illustration:} Scheduling data provider outage.\par
\section{Acceptance check}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Are all material assertions traceable to dated evidence?
\item Are alternatives and risks included?
\item Is the responsible reviewer identified?
\item Is the proceed/revise/stop decision supported?
\end{enumerate}
