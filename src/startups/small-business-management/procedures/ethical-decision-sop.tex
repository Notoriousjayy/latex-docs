\section{Control header}
\textbf{Process owner:} Founder or delegated ethics owner\par\textbf{Trigger:} new task or scheduled review; immediate escalation when the defined risk threshold is exceeded.\par\textbf{Records:} dated input, decision, exceptions, approval and revision history.\par\textbf{Applicability:} fictional examples are educational; adapt this procedure to actual jurisdictions and contracts.
\section{Execution steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=8pt]
\item \textbf{Capture disputed action.} Identify the marketing, staffing, product or data decision in question.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Identify all affected parties.} List benefits, harms, rights, expectations and conflicts.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Check constraints.} Review contracts, applicable law and professional obligations.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Generate alternatives.} Include no-action and reversible alternatives.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Decide and disclose.} Record rationale, authority and required communication.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Track correction.} Audit claims, compensate or correct where appropriate, close evidence.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Exception management}
For a failed verification, halt irreversible commitments when practical, log the exception, define containment, assign action owner and due date, and seek approval from the accountable decision maker. A verbal decision does not replace retained evidence.
\section{Review and improvement}
At every recurring review evaluate cycle time, deviations, leading indicators, customer consequence, corrective actions and control effectiveness. Retire controls that do not reduce meaningful risk; revise when constraints change.
