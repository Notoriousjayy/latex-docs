\section{Control header}
\textbf{Process owner:} People operations owner\par\textbf{Trigger:} new task or scheduled review; immediate escalation when the defined risk threshold is exceeded.\par\textbf{Records:} dated input, decision, exceptions, approval and revision history.\par\textbf{Applicability:} fictional examples are educational; adapt this procedure to actual jurisdictions and contracts.
\section{Execution steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=8pt]
\item \textbf{Authorize role.} Document need, approved pay budget, essential competencies and supervisor.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Apply consistent evaluation.} Use job-related structured questions and comparable rubrics.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Check employment requirements.} Verify jurisdiction-specific legal rules with qualified resources.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Grant least privilege.} Record approved accounts, equipment and confidentiality training.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Train core operations.} Require supervised demonstration of critical tasks.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Review 30-day performance.} Document coaching, access correction and confirmation or escalation.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Exception management}
For a failed verification, halt irreversible commitments when practical, log the exception, define containment, assign action owner and due date, and seek approval from the accountable decision maker. A verbal decision does not replace retained evidence.
\section{Review and improvement}
At every recurring review evaluate cycle time, deviations, leading indicators, customer consequence, corrective actions and control effectiveness. Retire controls that do not reduce meaningful risk; revise when constraints change.
