\section{Control header}
\textbf{Process owner:} Finance owner\par\textbf{Trigger:} new task or scheduled review; immediate escalation when the defined risk threshold is exceeded.\par\textbf{Records:} dated input, decision, exceptions, approval and revision history.\par\textbf{Applicability:} fictional examples are educational; adapt this procedure to actual jurisdictions and contracts.
\section{Execution steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=8pt]
\item \textbf{Lock cut-off.} Document reporting dates and complete source ledger extracts.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Reconcile balances.} Agree bank activity, receivables, payables and inventory with records.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Prepare statements.} Reconcile income, balance sheet and cash-flow bridge.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Analyze variance.} Compare actuals with baseline drivers, not only totals.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Refresh liquidity.} Update weekly cash forecast, cash floor and funding decisions.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\item \textbf{Review exceptions.} Approve or correct unreconciled items and archive evidence.\par\textit{Verification:} retain dated evidence and state whether the condition passed, failed, or needs remediation.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Exception management}
For a failed verification, halt irreversible commitments when practical, log the exception, define containment, assign action owner and due date, and seek approval from the accountable decision maker. A verbal decision does not replace retained evidence.
\section{Review and improvement}
At every recurring review evaluate cycle time, deviations, leading indicators, customer consequence, corrective actions and control effectiveness. Retire controls that do not reduce meaningful risk; revise when constraints change.
