\section{Scope and attribution}
Chapters 1--23 supply the subject structure. This reference supplies original implementation methods and example controls. Statements about current law, government programs, financing terms and reporting requirements require independent verification before practical use.
\section{Segmentation}
Define target customers by observed needs, willingness to pay, context, reachability and cost to serve; avoid using an enormous market estimate as a demand forecast.
\section{Value proposition}
State target customer, current costly alternative, improvement, evidence and conditions. Avoid unsupported guaranteed outcomes.
\section{Sales funnel}
Document qualified reach, leads, sales conversations, proposals, purchases, activation and repeat purchase; each conversion rate needs a denominator and observation window.
\section{Acquisition economics}
Customer acquisition cost is acquisition spend divided by newly acquired customers in a matching period; contribution payback requires realized contribution rather than gross sales.
\section{CRM and service}
Record contact purpose, consent/legal basis where applicable, support case priority, closure evidence, feedback and corrective action; minimize retained customer data.
\section{Pricing}
Use cost floor, customer-perceived value and competitor context as distinct inputs, then check contribution economics and demand sensitivity.
\section{Promotion testing}
Pre-register audience, proposition, spend limit, outcome, review date and stop condition; compare alternatives where practical.
\section{Product and suppliers}
Treat a roadmap as hypotheses; a new feature or supplier must have quality, capability, cost and continuity evidence.
\section{International expansion}
Validate language, contracts, tax, privacy, payment, customs and sanctions exposure with qualified advice; textbook international references may be dated.
\section{Assurance review}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Name the decision maker and identify relevant input evidence.
\item State assumptions and test conditions in falsifiable terms.
\item Compute or inspect the acceptance criteria before approving the stage.
\item Record residual uncertainty and a next-review trigger.
\end{enumerate}
