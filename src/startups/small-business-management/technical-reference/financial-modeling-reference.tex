\section{Scope and attribution}
Chapters 1--23 supply the subject structure. This reference supplies original implementation methods and example controls. Statements about current law, government programs, financing terms and reporting requirements require independent verification before practical use.
\section{Three statements}
Income statement measures profit over a period, balance sheet records assets and claims at a point in time, and cash-flow statement explains cash movement. Net income is not ending cash.
\section{Accounting identities}
$\mathrm{Assets}=\mathrm{Liabilities}+\mathrm{Equity}$. Cross-foot balance-sheet movements and reconcile opening plus net cash movement to closing cash.
\section{Contribution economics}
$CM=p-v$ per unit; $CMR=(p-v)/p$; break-even units $Q_{BE}=F/(p-v)$ for constant unit selling price, unit variable cost and period fixed cost.
\section{Forecast driver tree}
Sales = customers times purchase frequency times average transaction size. Collections = invoiced sales adjusted for payment timing, refunds and bad-debt losses. Preserve scenario provenance.
\section{Thirteen-week liquidity}
For each week: ending cash = opening cash + collections + financing inflows - supplier payments - payroll - tax and other payments - debt service. Roll the ending cash forward as next opening cash.
\section{Cash conversion}
$CCC=DIO+DSO-DPO$, where inventory days, receivables days and payables days use consistent average balances and related annual flow denominators.
\section{Investment decisions}
$NPV=-I_0+\sum_{t=1}^{T}CF_t/(1+r)^t$. Specify discount rate, time unit, tax convention, working-capital changes and terminal value separately.
\section{Model assurance}
Flag negative cash, balance-sheet mismatches, zero/negative contribution, implausible margins, changing tax assumptions and untraceable hard-coded forecast totals.
\section{Stress testing}
Vary gross margin, sales delay, churn, inventory lead time and collections lag independently. Measure the earliest minimum-cash breach and recovery action.
\section{Accounting boundary}
These are instructional simplifications, not an accounting-policy election or substitute for a licensed accountant.
\section{Assurance review}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Name the decision maker and identify relevant input evidence.
\item State assumptions and test conditions in falsifiable terms.
\item Compute or inspect the acceptance criteria before approving the stage.
\item Record residual uncertainty and a next-review trigger.
\end{enumerate}
