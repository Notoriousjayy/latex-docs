\section{Scope and attribution}
Chapters 1--23 supply the subject structure. This reference supplies original implementation methods and example controls. Statements about current law, government programs, financing terms and reporting requirements require independent verification before practical use.
\section{Opportunity discovery}
Log problems observed, workarounds, buyer and end-user roles. A problem without a reachable customer is not an investable market.
\section{Segment and offer}
Define a primary segment and the measurable outcome; list substitutes as well as direct competitors.
\section{Feasibility}
Evaluate market access, founder capabilities, economic feasibility, regulatory exposure and irreversible commitments.
\section{Business model}
Connect customer, offer, channel, revenue, delivery cost, partner dependence and retention in one causal model.
\section{Business plan}
Present evidence for market, execution, team, financial need and risks; use an assumption register rather than rhetorical certainty.
\section{Funding and governance}
Decide control rights, liability, information responsibilities and debt service before taking funds.
\section{Launch}
Require payment collection, customer support, security, supplier arrangements and change controls, not merely a marketing announcement.
\section{Growth}
Expand only after checking unit economics, operating capacity and working capital; measure cohorts not just total sales.
\section{Harvest and continuity}
Keep ownership, customer contracts, operating manuals and succession arrangements transferable.
\section{Decision gate contract}
Record objective, evidence ID, known limitations, alternatives, decision maker, verdict, review date and reversal trigger.
\section{Assurance review}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Name the decision maker and identify relevant input evidence.
\item State assumptions and test conditions in falsifiable terms.
\item Compute or inspect the acceptance criteria before approving the stage.
\item Record residual uncertainty and a next-review trigger.
\end{enumerate}
