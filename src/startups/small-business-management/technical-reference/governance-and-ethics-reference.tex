\section{Scope and attribution}
Chapters 1--23 supply the subject structure. This reference supplies original implementation methods and example controls. Statements about current law, government programs, financing terms and reporting requirements require independent verification before practical use.
\section{Stakeholder duties}
Map owners, employees, customers, suppliers, lenders, community and regulators. Record material harms and potential conflicts before deciding.
\section{Integrity}
Require supportable marketing claims, accurate financial disclosures and defined responsibility for corrective action.
\section{Formation}
Compare legal form on liability, management rights, transferability, taxation and capital access; obtain jurisdiction-specific legal and tax review.
\section{Governance}
Document decision rights, delegation limits, conflict disclosures, meeting cadence and escalation process. Advisory boards are not automatically formal directors.
\section{Franchising and contracting}
Review fees, termination, territory, contractor classification, intellectual-property license and control rights before committing.
\section{Family business}
Separate family expectations from employment conditions and company governance; document succession and buyout mechanisms.
\section{Employment}
Maintain consistent job-related selection criteria, access control and role training evidence. Wage, discrimination and classification rules must be checked against current law.
\section{Control environment}
Use dual approval for material disbursements, access reviews, vendor validation and segregation of duties where feasible.
\section{Professional boundary}
The textbook is an educational source. Legal, employment, regulatory and tax specifics change; verify current law before acting.
\section{Assurance review}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Name the decision maker and identify relevant input evidence.
\item State assumptions and test conditions in falsifiable terms.
\item Compute or inspect the acceptance criteria before approving the stage.
\item Record residual uncertainty and a next-review trigger.
\end{enumerate}
