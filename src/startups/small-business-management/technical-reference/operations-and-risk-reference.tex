\section{Scope and attribution}
Chapters 1--23 supply the subject structure. This reference supplies original implementation methods and example controls. Statements about current law, government programs, financing terms and reporting requirements require independent verification before practical use.
\section{Process map}
Identify demand trigger, inputs, process steps, resource constraints, failure conditions, outputs, owner and customer acceptance criteria.
\section{Capacity}
Compare available service hours, task time, rework and variable demand. A high utilization percentage can increase waiting time and fragility.
\section{Service and manufacturing}
Distinguish intangible service contact/recovery from physical stock, work-in-progress, changeovers and supplier lead times.
\section{Quality control}
Track defects by type, customer severity and root cause; prefer error prevention and process correction to inspection-only solutions.
\section{Purchasing}
Approve suppliers on capability, quality, delivery, cost, business continuity and contract fit. Review single-point-of-failure dependencies.
\section{Inventory}
Separate reorder trigger, safety stock, economic trade-offs and cash tied in slow-moving inventory. Check the denominator for turnover.
\section{People and management}
Make delegation explicit: decision authority, operating measure, escalation threshold, review cycle and fallback owner.
\section{Risk lifecycle}
Identify exposure, estimate likelihood and consequence, choose avoid/reduce/transfer/accept, fund response, track residual risk and test recovery.
\section{Continuity}
Define maximum tolerable outage, alternate supplier, backups, customer notification owner and practice cadence; insurance complements but does not replace controls.
\section{Decision thresholds}
An untested backup, unconfirmed vendor claim or unowned incident response is a gap, not a passing control.
\section{Assurance review}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Name the decision maker and identify relevant input evidence.
\item State assumptions and test conditions in falsifiable terms.
\item Compute or inspect the acceptance criteria before approving the stage.
\item Record residual uncertainty and a next-review trigger.
\end{enumerate}
