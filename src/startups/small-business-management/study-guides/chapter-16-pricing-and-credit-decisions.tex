\section{Learning outcome}
Calculate break-even volume and assess risk from offering customers credit.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 10, 11, 15.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item cost and demand pricing
\item contribution margin
\item break-even analysis
\item pricing strategy
\item trade credit and receivables
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Unit contribution is price less variable cost; break-even divides fixed cost by unit contribution, assuming consistent product mix and cost behavior.
\item Markup on cost and gross margin on price are different percentages. Use a declared denominator whenever comparing pricing alternatives.
\item Customer credit can stimulate purchases while creating collection delays, losses and financing requirements. Credit policy is a liquidity decision.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Compare three prices with contribution, demand and collection assumptions.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Pricing and credit policy. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Contribution margin, customer impact, collections and defaults.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Using markup as if it were margin
\item Ignoring returns and bad debt
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
When can a higher revenue price produce lower cash contribution?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
