\section{Learning outcome}
Connect strategic choices today to future business transfer or exit possibilities.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 8, 10, 12.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item exit planning
\item selling a firm
\item owner cash distributions
\item valuation and payment terms
\item successor planning
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Harvest planning matters before a sale: contracts, books, leadership continuity and customer concentration affect transferability and buyer confidence.
\item Enterprise value is not the same as cash received by selling owners; net debt, closing adjustments, fees, taxes and payment structure can change proceeds.
\item Owners should evaluate financial outcomes together with legacy, employment effects, ongoing obligations and personal next steps.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Write an exit-option map with readiness gaps and transfer dependencies.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Harvest strategy outline. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Transferability, documentation, buyer risk and professional review.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Assuming a valuation equals cash proceeds
\item Ignoring customer concentration and succession
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
How can a company be attractive to buyers while unattractive to its founder?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
