\section{Learning outcome}
Compare physical, home-based and online locations on full operating economics.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 6, 7.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item physical-site selection
\item facility requirements
\item home-based operations
\item e-commerce footprint
\item location-driven risks
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Location is an economic and service-design decision that includes accessibility, labor, infrastructure, competition, permits and commitment flexibility.
\item Home-based work reduces some fixed expenses but does not remove security, zoning, insurance, customer access or professional presentation concerns.
\item Online location shifts the decision toward distribution, fulfillment, reliability and trust; domain or storefront existence does not establish demand.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Score three candidate locations with fixed costs, access, constraints and flexibility.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Location and channel decision memo. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Whole-life operating cost, constraints and alternatives.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Ignoring zoning/permit dependencies
\item Assuming remote commerce has no location decisions
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
When is proximity worth higher fixed costs?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
