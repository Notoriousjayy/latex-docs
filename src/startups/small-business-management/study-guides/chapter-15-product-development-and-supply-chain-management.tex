\section{Learning outcome}
Evaluate product changes against segment demand, margin and supply feasibility.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 7, 14.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item growth tradeoffs
\item innovation process
\item product life cycle
\item branding and protection
\item suppliers and distribution
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Product innovation should respond to tested unmet needs and the product life cycle rather than novelty alone.
\item Brand, packaging, warranties and intellectual-property controls can add perceived and legal value but also create promises and compliance obligations.
\item Supplier and distribution designs determine lead times, quality exposure and cash tied in inventory; a low unit price can create high total risk.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Build a stage-gated product change plan and supplier scorecard.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Product and supplier strategy. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Quality, legal review, inventory, lead time and continuity.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Adding features without validated demand
\item Creating single-supplier dependence
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
What evidence supports delaying a promising product launch?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
