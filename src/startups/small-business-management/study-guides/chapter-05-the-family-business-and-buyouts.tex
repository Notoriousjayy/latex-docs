\section{Learning outcome}
Structure a buyout review that separates enterprise value from family expectations.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 3, 4.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item family and business overlap
\item governance and succession
\item management roles
\item acquisition due diligence
\item valuation and negotiation
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Family involvement introduces governance and relationship conflicts that do not disappear merely because owners share values or a surname.
\item Succession has distinct management and ownership dimensions; a successor may be capable of one but not entitled or equipped for the other.
\item Acquisition due diligence must reconcile economic earnings, balance-sheet claims, transferable customers, contingent liabilities and key-person dependence.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Develop a seller-question list, succession risks and a normalized earnings bridge.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Buyout and succession decision memorandum. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Verified books, quality of earnings, and asset/legal due diligence.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Confusing owner compensation with free cash flow
\item Ignoring succession dependencies
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
What would cause you to reject a profitable acquisition?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
