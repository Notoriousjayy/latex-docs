\section{Learning outcome}
Evaluate liquidity and asset investments using cash conversion and discounted cash flow.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 10, 11, 16, 21.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item working capital cycle
\item cash discipline
\item receivables
\item inventory and payables
\item capital budgeting
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Working capital decisions connect inventory, receivables, payables and cash; improving one component may damage service reliability or supplier relations.
\item The cash conversion cycle estimates the timing gap between paying for operations and collecting from customers under stated assumptions.
\item Capital budgeting compares incremental cash flows over time, often using NPV and sensitivity, while keeping nonfinancial risk and reversibility visible.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Compute a cash conversion cycle and compare capital investments using NPV.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Working capital and capex workbook. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Cash visibility, receivables aging, working capital and discount assumptions.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Reducing inventory without checking service levels
\item Using undiscounted profits for investment decisions
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
How can faster inventory turnover create a stockout risk?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
