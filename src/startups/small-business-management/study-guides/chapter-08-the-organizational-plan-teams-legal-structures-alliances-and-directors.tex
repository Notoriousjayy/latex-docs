\section{Learning outcome}
Identify key organizational decisions and their unresolved legal/tax assumptions.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 6.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item management team composition
\item entity forms
\item liability and control
\item strategic alliances
\item directors and advisors
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Entity selection affects liability, ownership, control, financing and tax treatment; no universal business form is optimal across jurisdictions.
\item A complementary management team closes operating skill gaps, but only when roles, decision rights and conflicts are documented.
\item Strategic alliances can supply distribution and capabilities while introducing concentration, intellectual property and dependency risk.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Produce a roles matrix and entity comparison memo for advisor review.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Organization and governance plan. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Accountable owners, separation of duties, professional review.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Choosing entity forms based on generic tax claims
\item Allocating ownership without written terms
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
What authority should a founder delegate first and why?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
