\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item A franchise exchanges autonomy and continuing payments for a defined business system, brand rights and potential support; due diligence must test whether promised support is realizable.
\item Franchise cash flow should include up-front rights, ongoing royalties, mandated purchases, marketing contributions and exit or renewal costs.
\item Contractor operating flexibility does not alone determine labor classification. Actual control, economics and applicable law require current legal review.
\end{itemize}

\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Ignoring royalty and territory restrictions
\item Treating contractor status as universally fixed
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
When does brand support justify loss of operational discretion?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
