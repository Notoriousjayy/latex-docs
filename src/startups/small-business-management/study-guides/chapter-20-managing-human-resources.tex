\section{Learning outcome}
Specify a hiring and training flow with fair, job-relevant evidence.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 8, 19.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item recruitment and job descriptions
\item selection and evaluation
\item employee onboarding
\item training and benefits
\item employment compliance
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Recruitment quality begins with an accurate job analysis, essential skills and realistic budget rather than an attractive title.
\item Structured, job-related candidate evaluation improves comparability and evidence quality; legal restrictions and fair hiring duties require current review.
\item Onboarding is complete only when the worker demonstrates key tasks, understands escalation and has appropriate access and supervision.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Draft a role description, interview rubric and 30-day onboarding plan.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Hiring and onboarding packet. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Job relevance, authorization, training evidence and counsel review.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Informal or inconsistent candidate evaluation
\item Using obsolete guidance as current employment law
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
How do you verify that a new employee can perform critical tasks safely?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
