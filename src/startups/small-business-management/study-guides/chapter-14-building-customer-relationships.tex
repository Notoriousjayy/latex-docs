\section{Learning outcome}
Define a retention system in which customer evidence drives service improvement.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 7.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item customer relationship management
\item service quality
\item CRM technology
\item customer data and privacy
\item buyer decision behavior
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item CRM is a decision system that connects customer information, service behavior and retention, not merely a software subscription.
\item Recovering from service failure requires detection, ownership, remedy and systemic prevention; a superficial satisfaction metric is insufficient.
\item Customer data has costs and risks. Collect information for defined purposes, restrict access and review retention and consent requirements.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Design an onboarding, support and customer-feedback operating loop.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Customer relationship operating plan. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Privacy, response ownership, service recovery and churn evidence.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Gathering unnecessary customer data
\item Measuring satisfaction without acting on failures
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
Which service failure deserves a systemic fix rather than an apology alone?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
