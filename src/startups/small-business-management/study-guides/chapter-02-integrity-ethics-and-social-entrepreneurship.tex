\section{Learning outcome}
Use a reproducible ethical decision process for a conflict between growth, privacy and stakeholder expectations.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 1.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item integrity and stakeholder obligations
\item customers, owners and employees
\item ethical decision processes
\item ethical culture
\item social and environmental performance
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Integrity is an operating property: incentives, truthful statements, escalation paths and monitoring must agree with the values that leadership announces.
\item Stakeholder analysis distinguishes who bears cost from who receives benefit, preventing owner outcomes from hiding harm to employees, customers or communities.
\item Triple-bottom-line thinking tests financial performance beside people and environmental effects without assuming that any single measure proves ethical conduct.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Create an ethics charter and assess an exaggerated product claim before launch.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Stakeholder ethics and integrity charter. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Consequence mapping, truthful claims, and escalation owner.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Claiming unsupported product results
\item Reducing ethics to legal minimums
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
If sales growth requires omitting a material limitation, which stakeholders bear the risk?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
