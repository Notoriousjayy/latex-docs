\section{Learning outcome}
Compare foreign-market entry modes against cost, control and exposure.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 8, 9, 12, 15.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item international market drivers
\item export/import options
\item licensing and alliances
\item political and currency risk
\item cross-border assistance
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item International expansion can increase market access or resource options but introduces legal, political, currency, payment and logistics uncertainty.
\item Exporting, licensing, franchising, alliance and overseas facility choices trade control against investment and local operating complexity.
\item A pilot must verify customer demand, contract enforceability, payments and compliance before scaling cross-border commitments.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Create a country screening matrix for a fictional digital service.\par\textbf{Required submission.} International expansion screening. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Compliance review, currency, logistics and contingency.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Mistaking translation for localization
\item Ignoring payment, tax, privacy and sanctions questions
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
Which international entry assumption is least reversible?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
