\section{Learning outcome}
Define how a founder transitions from doing tasks to managing repeatable outcomes.
\section{Prerequisites}
Read chapters 8, 14, 15.\par\textbf{Required baseline:} ability to record assumptions and distinguish observations from opinions.
\section{Chapter concept map}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item leadership and culture
\item growth-stage management
\item planning and control
\item delegation and communication
\item time management
\end{itemize}

\section{Technical study notes}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item The founder-to-manager transition replaces personally executing everything with designing roles, information flow, standards and review loops.
\item Delegation without decision authority, resources and escalation triggers is not effective delegation; accountability must be observable.
\item A management cadence needs leading indicators, exception review and corrective actions rather than an endless collection of status meetings.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading and analysis protocol}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Read the chapter learning objectives, then mark terms and claims that need primary evidence.
\item For each chapter theme, distinguish the entrepreneur decision, information required, parties affected and failure condition.
\item Write a one-paragraph synthesis in your own words; cite the textbook chapter and separate contemporary practice notes.
\item Complete the applied activity and attach the underlying evidence, calculations or interview log.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Worked application and deliverable}
\textbf{Business exercise.} Create a weekly management cadence and delegation matrix.\par\textbf{Required submission.} Management operating rhythm. Include a dated assumption register and an explicit proceed/revise/stop recommendation.\par\textbf{Assurance checks.} Owner, decision rights, performance review and corrective action.
\section{Common reasoning failures}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Creating managerial bottlenecks
\item Assigning responsibility without authority
\end{itemize}
\noindent\textbf{Correction:} require counterevidence and identify the responsible decision-maker before approving the next stage.
\section{Discussion prompt}
What evidence indicates a founder is the main constraint on growth?\par\medskip\textbf{Evidence expected:} at least two alternatives, one quantitative or independently checkable observation, a trade-off, and a decision threshold.
\section{Exit assessment}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=5pt]
\item Explain each term from the concept map without copying the textbook.
\item Defend the applied decision using an assumption, evidence and counterargument.
\item Show an objective test or inspection that could prove a key assumption wrong.
\item State what would change the recommendation and who has approval authority.
\end{enumerate}
