\section{Evidence conventions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
\item Label documents as hypothesis, observed, projected, independently checked or approved.
\item Separate financial projection inputs from audited or actual transaction data.
\item Annotate decisions with version, accountable owner, date and next-review trigger.
\item Never interpret classroom scorecards as legal, accounting, lender or insurance approval.
\end{itemize}
