\docsection{Review setup and decision rules}
This original checklist operationalizes V\&B chapter 11 and the suite methodology. Review a fixed baseline with identified stakeholders. Assign an architecture reviewer, domain experts and facilitator; capture questions in advance. Reviewers examine the artifacts, not the author. Record each check as pass, fail, not verified or conditional not applicable with evidence and an owner; use the separate conformance guide for normative tests.

Minor & Usability or clarity defect with bounded impact.\\
