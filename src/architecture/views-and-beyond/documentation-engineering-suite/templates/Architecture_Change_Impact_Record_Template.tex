\DocProvenance
\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Revision} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Owner} \\
\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\section{Change proposal and trigger}
Declare proposed modification, driver, source revision and affected release.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Affected elements and relations}
List module, runtime and allocation element IDs with link type.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Affected interfaces and behavior}
Identify API/schema/state/timing contract modifications and consumers.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Decision and quality implications}
Identify ADRs to revisit, risks and scenarios to rerun.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Rollout, reversibility and evidence}
Document migration path, checkpoints, rollback conditions and monitoring.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Approval and residual risk}
List reviewers, final decision, deferred work and evidence links.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
