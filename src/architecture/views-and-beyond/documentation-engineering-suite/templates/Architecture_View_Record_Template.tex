\DocProvenance
\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Revision} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Owner} \\
\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\section{View identification and intended questions}
Name the view, style/viewpoint ID, scope, audience, system and quality questions.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Primary presentation}
Provide a diagram/representation and precise legend, version and notation rules.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Elements and properties}
Create element IDs, types, responsibilities, interfaces and meaningful properties.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Relations and properties}
Record each relation type, direction, constraint, protocol and exceptional paths.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Element interfaces}
Specify provided/required interfaces, contracts and dependency assumptions.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Element behavior}
Describe interaction traces, state changes, concurrency or temporal assumptions where relevant.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Context diagram}
Show internal boundary, external entities and out-of-scope relationships.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Variability guide}
Capture variable components, alternatives, constraints and configuration mechanisms.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Rationale and decisions}
Explain critical design trade-offs, ADR references and unresolved risks.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Correspondence and verification}
List cross-view links, validation evidence, change triggers and review owner.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
