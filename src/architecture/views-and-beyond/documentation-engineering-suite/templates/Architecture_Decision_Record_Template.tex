\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Revision} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Owner} \\
\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\section{Decision identity and status}
Assign unique ID, author, date, status and supersedes/superseded-by links.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Decision context and drivers}
Specify triggering concerns, requirements, architectural assumptions and constraints.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Options considered}
Compare at least two defensible options and the do-nothing baseline when appropriate.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Selection and rationale}
Explain choice using explicit criteria and trade-offs rather than taste.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Consequences and failure modes}
Identify positive and negative effects, operational costs and new risks.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Validation and revisit trigger}
Provide how the choice can be tested and what observations warrant reconsideration.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Traceability references}
Link concerns, scenarios, affected views, modules, runtime resources and evidence.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
