\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule

\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\section{Scenario identity and concern}
Name scenario, stakeholder, quality driver and related requirement.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Stimulus source and stimulus}
Explain who or what acts and the condition introduced.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Environment and affected artifact}
Specify operating mode, load, failure context and element targeted.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Required response}
Describe observable behavior rather than preferred implementation.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Response measure and threshold}
Define quantifiable criteria, units, measurement window and population.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Test design and evidence}
Define experiment, acceptance rule, instrumentation, links and decision owner.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
