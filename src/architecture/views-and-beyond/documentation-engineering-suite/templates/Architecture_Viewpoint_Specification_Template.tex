\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule

\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section{Viewpoint identity and version}
Give reusable viewpoint ID, status, stewardship and applicability scope.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Stakeholders and concerns}
Specify stakeholders, questions, concerns and associated quality attributes.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Model kinds}
Define the categories of model that must/may be used to construct a view.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Element types}
State membership rules, properties, allowed abstractions and identifier scheme.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Relation types}
Describe semantics, cardinality, direction and permitted properties.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Notations and legends}
Specify representation conventions, diagram grammar and textual supplements.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Correspondence rules}
Define how this viewpoint links to other viewpoints and how conflicts are detected.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Analysis and methods}
Describe supported reasoning, constraints and appropriate verification techniques.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Completeness and consistency rules}
Write testable acceptance criteria and cases that should fail validation.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Viewpoint evolution}
Record versioning, ownership, backward compatibility and known limitations.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
