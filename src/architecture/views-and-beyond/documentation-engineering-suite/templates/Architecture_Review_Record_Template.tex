\section{When to use this artifact}
Use this form to document one controlled unit of architectural evidence. The template contains instructional prompts. The companion blank is deliberately unfilled and suitable for printing or handwritten completion.

\section{Identity fields}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}YYYY@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Revision} & \textbf{Status} & \textbf{Owner} \\
\midrule
Example: ARCH-001 & 0.1 & Draft & Architecture lead \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\normalsize
\section{Review mission and intended use}
State what job the document must support, stakeholders and acceptance bar.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Review subject and baseline}
Record exact artifact revisions, exclusions and evidence corpus.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Review questions and method}
Include explicit topic questions, inspection and corroboration approach.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Findings and reproducible evidence}
Use IDs, location, finding, consequence, severity and suggested resolution.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Dispositions and ownership}
Assign responsible owner, status, decision, deadline and approved exceptions.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Follow-up and release decision}
Record retest, residual risk, signoffs and next review trigger.

\BlankLines{2}
\section{Template completion checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item Identifiers are stable and references resolve.
\item Every materially important claim has a source, reviewer, or explicit assumption.
\item No uncontrolled secrets, internal credentials, or sensitive data are published.
\item Status and decision authority are recorded.
\end{itemize}
