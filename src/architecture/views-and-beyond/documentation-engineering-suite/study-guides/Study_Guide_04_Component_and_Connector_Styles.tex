\section{Conceptual focus}
Style choice shapes reliability, coupling, communication semantics, and observable failure modes.

\section{Reading map}
Book location: Component-and-Connector Styles. Principal topics: pipes and filters, client-server, peer-to-peer, service-oriented, pub-sub, shared data. Read the chapter discussion, then its summary checklist and selected discussion questions. Record terms precisely before adapting them to an implementation.

\section{Learning objectives}
\begin{itemize}
\item Explain pipes and filters, client-server, peer-to-peer, service-oriented, pub-sub, shared data in terms of a stakeholder question.
\item Document rendezvous, ordering, durability, and consistency assumptions; compare synchronous and asynchronous patterns.
\item Separate book-supported architectural concepts from optional implementation techniques such as Terraform, OpenAPI, and CI-generated evidence.
\end{itemize}
