\section{Conceptual focus}
A notation is a communication instrument, not a substitute for viewpoint semantics and design evidence.

\section{Reading map}
Book location: Appendices A--C. Principal topics: UML, SysML, AADL and notation selection. Read the chapter discussion, then its summary checklist and selected discussion questions. Record terms precisely before adapting them to an implementation.

\section{Learning objectives}
\begin{itemize}
\item Explain UML, SysML, AADL and notation selection in terms of a stakeholder question.
\item Select representations based on analyzability, reader capability, precision and tooling.
\item Separate book-supported architectural concepts from optional implementation techniques such as Terraform, OpenAPI, and CI-generated evidence.
\end{itemize}
\section{Engineering interpretation}
Select representations based on analyzability, reader capability, precision and tooling.

\section{Worked laboratory assignment}
Represent one architecture concern using two notations and compare information loss.

\section{Evidence to submit}
Notation choice rationale + legend

\section{Review prompts}
\begin{itemize}
\item What makes a diagram interpretable without its author present?
\item What relation types or behavior are intentionally outside the scope of this view?
\item Which system change would invalidate your claims, and how would you detect it?
\end{itemize}
