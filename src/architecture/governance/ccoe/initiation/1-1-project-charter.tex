\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] If finished, on what date did it finish?
\item[2.] Who is the Cloud Center of Excellence project Manager?
\item[3.] Cloud Center of Excellence project background: what is the primary motivation for this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[4.] Pop quiz - which are the same inputs as in the Cloud Center of Excellence project charter?
\item[5.] Why Outsource?
\item[6.] How will you learn more about the process or system you are trying to improve?
\item[7.] When is a charter needed?
\item[8.] Why do you need to manage scope?
\item[9.] Customer: who are you doing the Cloud Center of Excellence project for?
\item[10.] Who are the stakeholders?
\item[11.] What is the justification?
\item[12.] What ideas do you have for initial tests of change (PDSA cycles)?
\item[13.] What is the purpose of the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[14.] How are Cloud Center of Excellence projects different from operations?
\item[15.] What are you trying to accomplish?
\item[16.] What is the most common tool for helping define the detail?
\item[17.] Name and describe the elements that deal with providing the detail?
\item[18.] What are you striving to accomplish (measurable goal(s))?
\item[19.] How will you know a change is an improvement?
\item[20.] Fit with other Products Compliments - Cannibalizes?
\item[21.] Why have you chosen the aim you have set forth?
\item[22.] What is the business need?
\item[23.] When will this occur?
\item[24.] What does it need to do?
\item[25.] For whom?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
