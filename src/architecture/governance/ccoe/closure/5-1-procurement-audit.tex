\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Does the contract include performance-based clauses?
\item[2.] Has alternatives been considered for the specified procurement Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[3.] Is there no evidence of any external or superior pressure to reach a specific result?
\item[4.] Are travel expenditures monitored to determine that they are in line with other employees and reasonable for the area of travel?
\item[5.] Was the pre-qualification screening for issue of tender documents done properly and in a fair manner?
\item[6.] Are buyers prohibited from accepting gifts from vendors?
\item[7.] Are risks managed to provide reasonable assurance regarding department procurement objectives?
\item[8.] Does the individual having check-signing responsibility review the use of the signature plates?
\item[9.] Are there reasonable procedures to identify possible sources of supply?
\item[10.] How do you address the risk of fraud and corruption?
\item[11.] How do you deal with budget constrains and assurance needs?
\item[12.] Are proper authorization and approval required prior to payment?
\item[13.] Are prices always included on the purchase order?
\item[14.] Does the procurement Cloud Center of Excellence project comply with European Communities regulations and rules?
\item[15.] Were results of the award procedures published?
\item[16.] Were technical requirements set strict enough to guarantee the desired performance without being unnecessarily tight to exclude favourable bids that do not comply with all requirements?
\item[17.] Were the tender documents comprehensive, transparent and non-discriminating?
\item[18.] Proper and complete records of transactions and events are maintained?
\item[19.] Relevance of the contract to the Internal Market?
\item[20.] Is there a formal program of inservice training for personnel in the business management function?
\item[21.] Are order quantities, deliveries and payment levels under the contract monitored by an appropriate official?
\item[22.] Is the opportunity properly published?
\item[23.] Are the established budget and timetable (milestones) respected?
\item[24.] Are proper financing arrangements taken?
\item[25.] Was your organization specific about the nature and scope of the performance before launching the procurement process?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
