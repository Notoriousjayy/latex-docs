\item[1.] Do you feel very comfortable with your prediction?
\item[2.] What conclusions/generalizations can you draw from this?
\item[3.] Activity: what is Missing?
\item[4.] Were there other ways you could have organized the data to achieve similar results?
\item[5.] Have you identified the Activity Leveling Priority code value on each activity?
\item[6.] Are the required resources available?
\item[7.] Is there a trend during the year?
\item[8.] Would you consider either of corresponding activities an outlier?
\item[9.] Does your organization of the data change its meaning?
\item[10.] Can more resources be added?
\item[11.] Which method produces the more accurate cost assignment?
\item[12.] Activity: fair or not fair?
\item[13.] Resource is assigned to?
\item[14.] What is the general pattern here?
\item[15.] What is missing?
\item[16.] Can you re-assign any activities to another resource to resolve an over-allocation?
\item[17.] Where else does it apply?
\item[18.] How many resources do you need to complete the work scope within a limit of X number of days?
\item[19.] Have constraints been applied to the start and finish milestones for the phases?
\item[20.] Resources to accomplish the work?
