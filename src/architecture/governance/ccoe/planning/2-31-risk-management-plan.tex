\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What can you do to minimize the impact if it does?
\item[2.] Financial risk: can your organization afford to undertake the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[3.] Who/what can assist?
\item[4.] Do requirements put excessive performance constraints on the product?
\item[5.] Methodology: how will risk management be performed on this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[6.] Monitoring -what factors can you track that will enable you to determine if the risk is becoming more or less likely?
\item[7.] Are there new risks that mitigation strategies might introduce?
\item[8.] Are requirements fully understood by the software engineering team and customers?
\item[9.] Was an original risk assessment/risk management plan completed?
\item[10.] Does the software engineering team have the right mix of skills?
\item[11.] Internal technical and management reviews?
\item[12.] How risk averse are you?
\item[13.] What things might go wrong?
\item[14.] Is the customer willing to establish rapid communication links with the developer?
\item[15.] What is the likelihood?
\item[16.] Have staff received necessary training?
\item[17.] Should the risk be taken at all?
\item[18.] What will the damage be?
\item[19.] Is a software Cloud Center of Excellence project management tool available?
\item[20.] Can you stabilize dynamic risk factors?
\item[21.] Has something like this been done before?
\item[22.] Are end-users enthusiastically committed to the Cloud Center of Excellence project and the system/product to be built?
\item[23.] Is the customer willing to participate in reviews?
\item[24.] Do requirements demand the use of new analysis, design, or testing methods?
\item[25.] What other risks are created by choosing an avoidance strategy?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
