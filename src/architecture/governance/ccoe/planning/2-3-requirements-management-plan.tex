\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Who will do the reporting and to whom will reports be delivered?
\item[2.] Are all the stakeholders ready for the transition into the user community?
\item[3.] Could inaccurate or incomplete requirements in this Cloud Center of Excellence project create a serious risk for the business?
\item[4.] Is the system software (non-operating system) new to the IT Cloud Center of Excellence project team?
\item[5.] Is requirements work dependent on any other specific Cloud Center of Excellence project or non-Cloud Center of Excellence project activities (e.g. funding, approvals, procurement)?
\item[6.] Is the change control process documented?
\item[7.] Is any organizational data being used or stored?
\item[8.] Did you use declarative statements?
\item[9.] To see if a requirement statement is sufficiently well-defined, read it from the developers perspective. Mentally add the phrase, call me when youre done to the end of the requirement and see if that makes you nervous. In other words, would you need additional clarification from the author to understand the requirement well enough to design and implement it?
\item[10.] Will you document changes to requirements?
\item[11.] Who will finally present the work or product(s) for acceptance?
\item[12.] Will the contractors involved take full responsibility?
\item[13.] Will you have access to stakeholders when you need them?
\item[14.] Who will perform the analysis?
\item[15.] What is the earliest finish date for this Cloud Center of Excellence project if it is scheduled to start on ...?
\item[16.] Controlling Cloud Center of Excellence project requirements involves monitoring the status of the Cloud Center of Excellence project requirements and managing changes to the requirements. Who is responsible for monitoring and tracking the Cloud Center of Excellence project requirements?
\item[17.] How will the information be distributed?
\item[18.] How will requirements be managed?
\item[19.] Who is responsible for quantifying the Cloud Center of Excellence project requirements?
\item[20.] When and how will a requirements baseline be established in this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[21.] In case of software development; Should you have a test for each code module?
\item[22.] Are actual resources expenditures versus planned expenditures acceptable?
\item[23.] How will you communicate scheduled tasks to other team members?
\item[24.] Did you get proper approvals?
\item[25.] Is stakeholder risk tolerance an important factor for the requirements process in this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
