\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] How might they respond to the message and if the response may be negative or open to misinterpretation, what else needs to be said?
\item[2.] What are the training strategies?
\item[3.] Has this been negotiated with the customer and sponsor?
\item[4.] Do there need to be new channels developed?
\item[5.] What new roles are needed?
\item[6.] Who will do the training?
\item[7.] What are the needs, priorities and special interests of the audience?
\item[8.] What do you expect the target audience to do, say, think or feel as a result of this communication?
\item[9.] Who might be able to help you the most?
\item[10.] Is there support for this application(s) and are the details available for distribution?
\item[11.] Identify the risk and assess the significance and likelihood of it occurring and plan the contingency What risks may occur upfront?
\item[12.] Has the training provider been established?
\item[13.] Who will fund the training?
\item[14.] Who might present the most resistance?
\item[15.] What goal(s) do you hope to accomplish?
\item[16.] What relationships will change?
\item[17.] What is the most positive interpretation it can receive?
\item[18.] What communication network would you use - informal or formal?
\item[19.] Have the business unit contacts been selected and notified?
\item[20.] Will the readiness criteria be met prior to the training roll out?
\item[21.] How does the principle of senders and receivers make the Cloud Center of Excellence project communications effort more complex?
\item[22.] Who will be the change levers?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
