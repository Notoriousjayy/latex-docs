\item[1.] Published materials?
\item[2.] Are staff skills known and available for each task?
\item[3.] Are status reports received per the Cloud Center of Excellence project Plan?
\item[4.] Does the Cloud Center of Excellence project have a formal Cloud Center of Excellence project Charter?
\item[5.] Has Cloud Center of Excellence project success criteria been defined?
\item[6.] Is there a procurement management plan in place?
\item[7.] Were Cloud Center of Excellence project team members involved in detailed estimating and scheduling?
\item[8.] What areas does the group agree are the biggest success on the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[9.] Is there any form of automated support for Issues Management?
\item[10.] How will multiple providers be managed?
\item[11.] Financial capacity; does the seller have, or can the seller reasonably be expected to obtain, the financial resources needed?
\item[12.] Does all Cloud Center of Excellence project documentation reside in a common repository for easy access?
\item[13.] Has your organization readiness assessment been conducted?
\item[14.] Are risk oriented checklists used during risk identification?
\item[15.] How long will it take for the purchase cost to be the same as the lease cost?
\item[16.] Is a pmo (Cloud Center of Excellence project management office) in place which provides oversight to the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[17.] Is pert / critical path or equivalent methodology being used?
\item[18.] In which phase of the Acquisition Process Cycle does source qualifications reside?
\item[19.] Is Cloud Center of Excellence project status reviewed with the steering and executive teams at appropriate intervals?
\item[20.] Is there an issues management plan in place?
\item[21.] Is the current scope of the Cloud Center of Excellence project substantially different than that originally defined?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
