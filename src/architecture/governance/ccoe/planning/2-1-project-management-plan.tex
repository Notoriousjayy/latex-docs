\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Do the proposed changes from the Cloud Center of Excellence project include any significant risks to safety?
\item[2.] What are the constraints?
\item[3.] How do you manage time?
\item[4.] Are alternatives safe, functional, constructible, economical, reasonable and sustainable?
\item[5.] If the Cloud Center of Excellence project management plan is a comprehensive document that guides you in Cloud Center of Excellence project execution and control, then what should it NOT contain?
\item[6.] Was the peer (technical) review of the cost estimates duly coordinated with the cost estimate center of expertise and addressed in the review documentation and certification?
\item[7.] What goes into your Cloud Center of Excellence project Charter?
\item[8.] Is mitigation authorized or recommended?
\item[9.] Are there any scope changes proposed for a previously authorized Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[10.] Has the selected plan been formulated using cost effectiveness and incremental analysis techniques?
\item[11.] Who is the sponsor?
\item[12.] Is there anything you would now do differently on your Cloud Center of Excellence project based on past experience?
\item[13.] Where does all this information come from?
\item[14.] What data/reports/tools/etc. do program managers need?
\item[15.] Are there non-structural buyout or relocation recommendations?
\item[16.] Is there an incremental analysis/cost effectiveness analysis of proposed mitigation features based on an approved method and using an accepted model?
\item[17.] Will you add a schedule and diagram?
\item[18.] What are the assigned resources?
\item[19.] Who is the Cloud Center of Excellence project Manager?
\item[20.] Are the existing and future without-plan conditions reasonable and appropriate?
\item[21.] Are cost risk analysis methods applied to develop contingencies for the estimated total Cloud Center of Excellence project costs?
\item[22.] What are the training needs?
\item[23.] If the Cloud Center of Excellence project is complex or scope is specialized, do you have appropriate and/or qualified staff available to perform the tasks?
\item[24.] How can you best help your organization to develop consistent practices in Cloud Center of Excellence project management planning stages?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
