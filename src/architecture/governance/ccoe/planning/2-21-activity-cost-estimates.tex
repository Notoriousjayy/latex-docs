\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] One way to define activities is to consider how organization employees describe jobs to families and friends. You basically want to know, What do you do?
\item[2.] Where can you get activity reports?
\item[3.] What is included in indirect cost being allocated?
\item[4.] Which contract type places the most risk on the seller?
\item[5.] What do you want to know about the stay to know if costs were inappropriately high or low?
\item[6.] How many activities should you have?
\item[7.] Are data needed on characteristics of care?
\item[8.] Were decisions made in a timely manner?
\item[9.] What were things that you did very well and want to do the same again on the next Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[10.] What makes a good expected result statement?
\item[11.] When do you enter into PPM?
\item[12.] How do you do activity recasts?
\item[13.] What is a Cloud Center of Excellence project Management Plan?
\item[14.] What procedures are put in place regarding bidding and cost comparisons, if any?
\item[15.] What were things that you did well, and could improve, and how?
\item[16.] Can you delete activities or make them inactive?
\item[17.] How do you allocate indirect costs to activities?
\item[18.] Were escalated issues resolved promptly?
\item[19.] Certification of actual expenditures?
\item[20.] How do you manage cost?
\item[21.] What areas were overlooked on this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[22.] How Award?
\item[23.] What is Cloud Center of Excellence project cost management?
\item[24.] Why do you manage cost?
\item[25.] Does the estimator estimate by task or by person?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
