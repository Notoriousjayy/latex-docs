\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What is the industrial relations prevailing in this organization?
\item[2.] How well were you able to manage your risk?
\item[3.] Is there any sign of biased ranking?
\item[4.] What would you do differently?
\item[5.] To what extent is the chosen technology maturing?
\item[6.] Which risks need to move on to Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis?
\item[7.] Which is the BEST thing to do?
\item[8.] What should be the gestation period for the Cloud Center of Excellence project with this technology?
\item[9.] What will be the environmental impact of the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[10.] Workarounds are determined during which risk management process?
\item[11.] Do end-users have realistic expectations?
\item[12.] What action would you take to the identified risks in the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[13.] Are formal technical reviews part of this process?
\item[14.] What are the levels of understanding of the future users of this technology?
\item[15.] Does the customer understand the software process?
\item[16.] What are the preparations required for facing difficulties?
\item[17.] Do others match with the clients requirement?
\item[18.] Can you avoid altogether some things that might go wrong?
\item[19.] Are you working on the right risks?
\item[20.] What will be the impact or consequence if the risk occurs?
\item[21.] What new technologies are being explored in the same area?
\item[22.] Several experts are offsite, and wish to be included. How can this be done?
\item[23.] Will there be an increase in the political conservatism?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
