\item[2.] How small is small enough?
\item[3.] Will you use a Requirements Traceability Matrix?
\item[4.] What is the WBS?
\item[5.] How do you manage scope?
\item[6.] Why do you manage scope?
\item[7.] Is there a requirements traceability process in place?
\item[8.] What percentage of Cloud Center of Excellence projects are producing traceability matrices between requirements and other work products?
\item[9.] How will it affect the stakeholders personally in career?
\item[10.] What are the chronologies, contingencies, consequences, criteria?
\item[11.] Why use a WBS?
\item[12.] Describe the process for approving requirements so they can be added to the traceability matrix and Cloud Center of Excellence project work can be performed. Will the Cloud Center of Excellence project requirements become approved in writing?
