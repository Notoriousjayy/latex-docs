\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What will be the consequences if the risk happens?
\item[2.] What do you know?
\item[3.] What do people affected think about the need for, and practicality of preventive measures?
\item[4.] Has a sensitivity analysis been carried out?
\item[5.] Will revised controls lead to tolerable risk levels?
\item[6.] How reliable is the data source?
\item[7.] During work activities could hazards exist?
\item[8.] How do you handle product safely?
\item[9.] Potential for recurrence?
\item[10.] What are you trying to achieve (Objectives)?
\item[11.] Has the most cost-effective solution been chosen?
\item[12.] If it happens, what are the consequences?
\item[13.] Whom do you serve (customers)?
\item[14.] What can happen?
\item[15.] Who has a vested interest in how you perform as your organization (our stakeholders)?
\item[16.] What is the environment within which you operate (social trends, economic, community values, broad based participation, national directions etc.)?
\item[17.] How can hazards be reduced?
\item[18.] Do effective diagnostic tests exist?
\item[19.] What were the Causes that contributed?
\item[20.] What are the main threats to your existence?
\item[21.] What is the likelihood of it happening?
\item[22.] What will be the consequences if it happens?
\item[23.] Type of risk identified?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
