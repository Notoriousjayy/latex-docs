\item[3.] Ranged estimates?
\item[4.] Do Cloud Center of Excellence project teams \& team members report on status / activities / progress?
\item[5.] Were Cloud Center of Excellence project team members involved in the development of activity \& task decomposition?
\item[6.] Have Cloud Center of Excellence project management standards and procedures been identified / established and documented?
\item[7.] Is the assigned Cloud Center of Excellence project manager a PMP (Certified Cloud Center of Excellence project manager) and experienced?
\item[8.] Weve met your goals?
\item[9.] Similar Cloud Center of Excellence projects?
\item[10.] Contractors scope - how will contractors scope be defined when contracts are let?
\item[11.] Is a stakeholder management plan in place that covers topics?
\item[12.] Are post milestone Cloud Center of Excellence project reviews (PMPR) conducted with your organization at least once a year?
\item[13.] Have external dependencies been captured in the schedule?
\item[14.] Has the scope management document been updated and distributed to help prevent scope creep?
\item[15.] Is the Cloud Center of Excellence project schedule available for all Cloud Center of Excellence project team members to review?
\item[16.] What strengths do you have?
\item[17.] Eac -estimate at completion, what is the total job expected to cost?
\item[18.] Have reserves been created to address risks?
\item[19.] Is your organization certified as a supplier, wholesaler, regular dealer, or manufacturer of corresponding products/supplies?
\item[20.] Was the scope definition used in task sequencing?
