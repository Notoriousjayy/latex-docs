\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Will the Cloud Center of Excellence project collaborate with the local community and leverage resources?
\item[2.] Who is best positioned to know and assist in identifying corresponding factors?
\item[3.] What costs are to be estimated?
\item[4.] What will others want?
\item[5.] Is it feasible to establish a control group arrangement?
\item[6.] How will the results be shared and to whom?
\item[7.] What additional Cloud Center of Excellence project(s) could be initiated as a result of this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[8.] Can a trend be established from historical performance data on the selected measure and are the criteria for using trend analysis or forecasting methods met?
\item[9.] Ask: are others positioned to know, are others credible, and will others cooperate?
\item[10.] What is the purpose of estimating?
\item[11.] Does the Cloud Center of Excellence project provide innovative ways for stakeholders to overcome obstacles or deliver better outcomes?
\item[12.] What can be included?
\item[13.] What happens to any remaining funds not used?
\item[14.] Identify the timeframe necessary to monitor progress and collect data to determine how the selected measure has changed?
\item[15.] Is the Cloud Center of Excellence project responsive to community need?
\item[16.] What is the estimated labor cost today based upon this information?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
