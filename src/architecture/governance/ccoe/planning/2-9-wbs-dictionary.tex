\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What size should a work package be?
\item[2.] Changes in the direct base to which overhead costs are allocated?
\item[3.] Does the scheduling system provide for the identification of work progress against technical and other milestones, and also provide for forecasts of completion dates of scheduled work?
\item[4.] Is work progressively subdivided into detailed work packages as requirements are defined?
\item[5.] Is budgeted cost for work performed calculated in a manner consistent with the way work is planned?
\item[6.] Does the contractors system provide for the determination of cost variances attributable to the excess usage of material?
\item[7.] Is all budget available as management reserve identified and excluded from the performance measurement baseline?
\item[8.] Does the accounting system provide a basis for auditing records of direct costs chargeable to the contract?
\item[9.] Are the latest revised estimates of costs at completion compared with the established budgets at appropriate levels and causes of variances identified?
\item[10.] Does the contractors system include procedures for measuring performance of the lowest level organization responsible for the control account?
\item[11.] Identify potential or actual budget-based and time-based schedule variances?
\item[12.] Are the wbs and organizational levels for application of the Cloud Center of Excellence projected overhead costs identified?
\item[13.] Are material costs reported within the same period as that in which BCWP is earned for that material?
\item[14.] Is each control account assigned to a single organizational element directly responsible for the work and identifiable to a single element of the CWBS?
\item[15.] Are the bases and rates for allocating costs from each indirect pool to commercial work consistent with the already stated used to allocate corresponding costs to Government contracts?
\item[16.] What is the goal?
\item[17.] What is wrong with this Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[18.] Major functional areas of contract effort?
\item[19.] Are authorized changes being incorporated in a timely manner?
\item[20.] Evaluate the performance of operating organizations?
\item[21.] Identify and isolate causes of favorable and unfavorable cost and schedule variances?
\item[22.] Are indirect costs accumulated for comparison with the corresponding budgets?
\item[23.] Are retroactive changes to BCWS and BCWP prohibited except for correction of errors or for normal accounting adjustments?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
