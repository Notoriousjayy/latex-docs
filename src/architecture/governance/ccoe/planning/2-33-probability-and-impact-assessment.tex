\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What is the likely future demand of the customer?
\item[2.] How will the consumption pattern change?
\item[3.] What are the probabilities of chosen technologies being suitable for local conditions?
\item[4.] What action do you usually take against risks?
\item[5.] Mitigation -how can you avoid the risk?
\item[6.] Is the present organizational structure for handling the Cloud Center of Excellence project sufficient?
\item[7.] Can the risk be avoided by choosing a different alternative?
\item[8.] Anticipated volatility of the requirements?
\item[9.] Do you manage the process through use of metrics?
\item[10.] Why has this particular mode of contracting been chosen?
\item[11.] Do you train all developers in the process?
\item[12.] Are flexibility and reuse paramount?
\item[13.] How is the Cloud Center of Excellence project going to be managed?
\item[14.] Are enough people available?
\item[15.] Risk categorization -which of your categories has more risk than others?
\item[16.] Are people attending meetings and doing work?
\item[17.] Is the delay in one subCloud Center of Excellence project going to affect another?
\item[18.] How are you working with risks?
\item[19.] What will be the likely political environment during the life of the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[20.] Will new information become available during the Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[21.] How much risk do others need to take?
\item[22.] Do you have a consistent repeatable process that is actually used?
\item[23.] Is the customer technically sophisticated in the product area?
\item[24.] Are the risk data complete?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
