\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] How do you consolidate reviews and analysis of evaluators?
\item[2.] How should the preproposal conference be conducted?
\item[3.] Is there collaboration among your evaluators?
\item[4.] Are responses to considerations adequate?
\item[5.] What should be the contracting officers strategy?
\item[6.] What is the basis of an estimate and what assumptions were made?
\item[7.] In order of importance, which evaluation criteria are the most critical to the determination of your overall rating?
\item[8.] What are the most common types of rating systems?
\item[9.] What evidence should be provided regarding proposal evaluations?
\item[10.] How can business terms and conditions be improved to yield more effective price competition?
\item[11.] Has all proposal data been loaded?
\item[12.] Do proposed hours support content and schedule?
\item[13.] How can the methods of publicizing the buy be tailored to yield more effective price competition?
\item[14.] Can you reasonably estimate total organization requirements for the coming year?
\item[15.] How should comments received in response to a RFP be handled?
\item[16.] How much weight should be placed on past performance information?
\item[17.] What source selection software is your team using?
\item[18.] Do you ensure you evaluate what you asked for, not what you want to see or expect to see?
\item[19.] How will you decide an evaluators write up is sufficient?
\item[20.] Do you have a plan to document consensus results including disposition of any disagreement by individual evaluators?
\item[21.] What are the steps in performing a cost/tech tradeoff?
\item[22.] What are the special considerations for preaward debriefings?
\item[23.] What benefits are accrued from issuing a DRFP in advance of issuing a final RFP?
\item[24.] What are the guiding principles for developing an evaluation report?
\item[25.] How will you evaluate offerors proposals?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
