\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What is the probability and impact of the risk occurring?
\item[2.] What are you going to do to limit the Cloud Center of Excellence projects risk exposure due to the identified risks?
\item[3.] Risk probability and impact: how will the probabilities and impacts of risk items be assessed?
\item[4.] What action, if any, has been taken to respond to the risk?
\item[5.] Can the likelihood and impact of failing to achieve corresponding recommendations and action plans be assessed?
\item[6.] Are there any gaps in the evidence?
\item[7.] What may happen or not go according to plan?
\item[8.] Budget and schedule: what are the estimated costs and schedules for performing risk-related activities?
\item[9.] What are your key risks/show istoppers and what is being done to manage them?
\item[10.] What are the main aims, objectives of the policy, strategy, or service and the intended outcomes?
\item[11.] When is it going to be done?
\item[12.] User involvement: do you have the right users?
\item[13.] Are implemented controls working as others should?
\item[14.] Assume the event happens, what is the Most Likely impact?
\item[15.] Amongst the action plans and recommendations that you have to introduce are there some that could stop or delay the overall program?
\item[16.] What evidence do you have to justify the likelihood score of the risk (audit, incident report, claim, complaints, inspection, internal review)?
\item[17.] Do you require further engagement?
\item[18.] What will be done?
\item[19.] What would the impact to the Cloud Center of Excellence project objectives be should the risk arise?
\item[20.] How could corresponding Risk affect the Cloud Center of Excellence project in terms of cost and schedule?
\item[21.] Which key risks have ineffective responses or outstanding improvement actions?
\item[22.] Are corrective measures implemented as planned?
\item[23.] Who needs to know about this?
\item[24.] What are the assumptions and current status that support the assessment of the risk?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
