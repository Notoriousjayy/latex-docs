\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Is the assigned Cloud Center of Excellence project manager a PMP (Certified Cloud Center of Excellence project manager) and experienced?
\item[2.] What is the unique product, service or result?
\item[3.] Are trade-offs between accepting the risk and mitigating the risk identified?
\item[4.] Why is a scope management plan important?
\item[5.] Have activity relationships and interdependencies within tasks been adequately identified?
\item[6.] Have key stakeholders been identified?
\item[7.] Are actuals compared against estimates to analyze and correct variances?
\item[8.] Are updated Cloud Center of Excellence project time \& resource estimates reasonable based on the current Cloud Center of Excellence project stage?
\item[9.] What is your organizations history in doing similar activities?
\item[10.] Has your organization done similar tasks before?
\item[11.] Are procurement deliverables arriving on time and to specification?
\item[12.] Are metrics used to evaluate and manage Vendors?
\item[13.] What threats might prevent you from getting there?
\item[14.] Are risk triggers captured?
\item[15.] What happens to rejected deliverables?
\item[16.] Describe how the deliverables will be verified against the Cloud Center of Excellence project scope. To whom will the deliverables be first presented for inspection and verification?
\item[17.] Will anyone else be involved in verifying the deliverables?
\item[18.] Are Cloud Center of Excellence project contact logs kept up to date?
\item[19.] Product - what are you trying to accomplish and how will you know when you are finished?
\item[20.] Have you identified possible roadblocks?
\item[21.] Has a Cloud Center of Excellence project Communications Plan been developed?
\item[22.] Have the key functions and capabilities been defined and assigned to each release or iteration?
\item[23.] Is quality monitored from the perspective of the customers needs and expectations?
\item[24.] What weaknesses do you have?
\item[25.] Does a documented Cloud Center of Excellence project organizational policy \& plan (i.e. governance model) exist?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
