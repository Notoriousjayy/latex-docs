\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Which are the right metrics to use?
\item[2.] What can manufacturing professionals do to ensure quality is seen as an integral part of the entire product lifecycle?
\item[3.] Have risk areas been identified?
\item[4.] Where is quality now?
\item[5.] Is material complete (and does it meet the standards)?
\item[6.] What are your organizations next steps?
\item[7.] What do you measure?
\item[8.] Is there a set of procedures to capture, analyze and act on quality metrics?
\item[9.] How do you calculate such metrics?
\item[10.] What group is empowered to define quality requirements?
\item[11.] Is quality culture a competitive advantage?
\item[12.] What happens if you get an abnormal result?
\item[13.] How does one achieve stability?
\item[14.] Was the overall quality better or worse than previous products?
\item[15.] Who is willing to lead?
\item[16.] What are your organizations expectations for its quality Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[17.] What is the timeline to meet your goal?
\item[18.] Were quality attributes reported?
\item[19.] How exactly do you define when differences exist?
\item[20.] Can visual measures help you to filter visualizations of interest?
\item[21.] Has risk analysis been adequately reviewed?
\item[22.] If the defect rate during testing is substantially higher than that of the previous release (or a similar product), then ask: Did you plan for and actually improve testing effectiveness?
\item[23.] What level of statistical confidence do you use?
\item[24.] Did evaluation start on time?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
