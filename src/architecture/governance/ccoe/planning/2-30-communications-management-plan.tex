\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] How were corresponding initiatives successful?
\item[2.] What data is going to be required?
\item[3.] Do you then often overlook a key stakeholder or stakeholder group?
\item[4.] What steps can you take for a positive relationship?
\item[5.] What communications method?
\item[6.] What is the political influence?
\item[7.] Why do you manage communications?
\item[8.] How much time does it take to do it?
\item[9.] What approaches do you use?
\item[10.] Who have you worked with in past, similar initiatives?
\item[11.] Are the stakeholders getting the information others need, are others consulted, are concerns addressed?
\item[12.] Which stakeholders are thought leaders, influences, or early adopters?
\item[13.] Are there too many who have an interest in some aspect of your work?
\item[14.] What approaches to you feel are the best ones to use?
\item[15.] What is the stakeholders level of authority?
\item[16.] Do you prepare stakeholder engagement plans?
\item[17.] Who were proponents/opponents?
\item[18.] Is there an important stakeholder who is actively opposed and will not receive messages?
\item[19.] Conflict resolution -which method when?
\item[20.] Why manage stakeholders?
\item[21.] Are you constantly rushing from meeting to meeting?
\item[22.] Do you ask; can you recommend others for you to talk with about this initiative?
\item[23.] Who needs to know and how much?
\item[24.] What does the stakeholder need from the team?
\item[25.] Do you feel a register helps?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
