\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Do technical resources exist?
\item[2.] If applicable; are there issues linked with the fact that this is an offshore Cloud Center of Excellence project?
\item[3.] How do you know when a Requirement is accurate enough?
\item[4.] What is effective documentation?
\item[5.] Are there legal issues?
\item[6.] Can the requirement be changed without a large impact on other requirements?
\item[7.] Basic work/business process; high-level, what is being touched?
\item[8.] How does the proposed Cloud Center of Excellence project contribute to the overall objectives of your organization?
\item[9.] Are there any requirements conflicts?
\item[10.] Has requirements gathering uncovered information that would necessitate changes?
\item[11.] How linear / iterative is your Requirements Gathering process (or will it be)?
\item[12.] Who is interacting with the system?
\item[13.] Can the requirements be checked?
\item[14.] What are the potential disadvantages/advantages?
\item[15.] How can you document system requirements?
\item[16.] Where are business rules being captured?
\item[17.] What will be the integration problems?
\item[18.] Is the origin of the requirement clearly stated?
\item[19.] What is the risk associated with cost and schedule?
\item[20.] Where do you define what is a customer, what are the attributes of customer?
\item[21.] Completeness. are all functions required by the customer included?
\item[22.] What happens when requirements are wrong?
\item[23.] The problem with gathering requirements is right there in the word gathering. What images does it conjure?
\item[24.] How will requirements be documented and who signs off on them?
\item[25.] Who provides requirements?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
