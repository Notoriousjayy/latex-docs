\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] To what degree are the structures of the formal organization consistent with the behaviors in the informal organization?
\item[2.] To what degree will team members, individually and collectively, commit time to help themselves and others learn and develop skills?
\item[3.] To what degree can the cognitive capacity of individuals accommodate the flow of information?
\item[4.] What degree are the relative importance and priority of the goals clear to all team members?
\item[5.] To what degree can all members engage in open and interactive considerations?
\item[6.] To what degree is the information network consistent with the structure of the formal organization?
\item[7.] To what degree do team members agree with the goals, relative importance, and the ways in which achievement will be measured?
\item[8.] To what degree does the formal organization make use of individual resources and meet individual needs?
\item[9.] To what degree do the structures of the formal organization motivate taskrelevant behavior and facilitate task completion?
\item[10.] To what degree do individual skills and abilities match task demands?
\item[11.] How will procurement be coordinated with other Cloud Center of Excellence project aspects, such as scheduling and performance reporting?
\item[12.] To what degree does the teams purpose constitute a broader, deeper aspiration than just accomplishing short-term goals?
\item[13.] To what degree do team members articulate the teams work approach?
\item[14.] To what degree is there centralized control of information sharing?
\item[15.] What is the degree to which rules govern information exchange between groups?
\item[16.] How is the data used?
\item[17.] To what degree will the team adopt a concrete, clearly understood, and agreed-upon approach that will result in achievement of the teams goals?
\item[18.] To what degree will each member have the opportunity to advance his or her professional skills in all three of the above categories while contributing to the accomplishment of the teams purpose and goals?
\item[19.] What is the degree to which rules govern information exchange between individuals within your organization?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
