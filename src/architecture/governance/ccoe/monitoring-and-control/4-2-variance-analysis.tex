\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What is the expected future profitability of each customer?
\item[2.] What is exceptional?
\item[3.] Are work packages assigned to performing organizations?
\item[4.] Can process improvements lead to unfavorable variances?
\item[5.] Do the rates and prices remain constant throughout the year?
\item[6.] When, during the last four quarters, did a primary business event occur causing a fluctuation?
\item[7.] Does the contractor use objective results, design reviews and tests to trace schedule performance?
\item[8.] The anticipated business volume?
\item[9.] Does the contractors system identify work accomplishment against the schedule plan?
\item[10.] Is all contract work included in the CWBS?
\item[11.] What business event caused the fluctuation?
\item[12.] Are data elements reconcilable between internal summary reports and reports forwarded to the stakeholders?
\item[13.] Are there externalities from having some customers, even if they are unprofitable in the short run?
\item[14.] What is the budgeted cost for work scheduled?
\item[15.] Budget versus actual. how does the monthly budget compare to actual experience?
\item[16.] Why do variances exist?
\item[17.] Are there changes in the overhead pool and/or organization structures?
\item[18.] Are the overhead pools formally and adequately identified?
\item[19.] How are material, labor, and overhead variances calculated and recorded?
\item[20.] What is the incurrence of actual indirect costs in excess of budgets, by element of expense?
\item[21.] How do you evaluate the impact of schedule changes, work around, et?
\item[22.] What are the actual costs to date?
\item[23.] Is the anticipated (firm and potential) business base Cloud Center of Excellence projected in a rational, consistent manner?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
