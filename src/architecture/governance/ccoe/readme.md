# Cloud Center of Excellence (CCoE) Governance

A reusable governance suite containing a master guide and 58 numbered, editable LaTeX forms across project-management process groups. The forms are reference and working artifacts, **not evidence of completed decisions or approvals**.

## Start here

Read the [master governance guide](ccoe-master-governance-guide.tex) for process relationships and tailoring. Select a source form from the numbered catalog, identify the actual owner and approver, and link populated records to project evidence. Use the [ADR library](../../decisions/readme.md) when a decision has architectural significance.

Planning, 10 Executing, 2 Monitoring and Controlling, and 4 Closing. The

## Governance relationships

- **Risk:** 2.31 establishes risk-management practice; 2.32 records risks,
  ownership, responses, status, and residual risk; 2.33 and 2.34 support the
  source-defined probability/impact assessment and prioritization; 2.35 records
  detailed risk information. 3.10 and 4.1 provide operational issue and
  performance follow-up. The source-defined scales and terms are retained.
- **Procurement:** 2.36 plans procurement and oversight; 2.37 supplies the
  source selection structure. Vendor performance and evidence connect to 5.1
  Procurement Audit and 5.2 Contract Close-Out. Do not populate supplier
  evaluations or decisions without project evidence.
- **Stakeholders:** 1.2 identifies stakeholders; 1.3 analyzes their
  characteristics; 2.38 plans engagement. 2.30 defines communications
  requirements and mechanisms supporting engagement, while 2.38 retains the
  stakeholder-specific communication-needs table and engagement approaches.
  2.27, 2.39, 3.4, and 4.1 provide related role, change, decision, and
  monitoring evidence. The plan does not duplicate the stakeholder register.
- **Change:** 2.39 defines change governance; 3.2 captures requests and impact
  assessment; 3.4 records approval decisions; 3.3 tracks approved changes
  through execution and closure. Reference affected requirements/baselines
  (including 2.5) and stakeholder engagement (2.38).
- **Procurement close-out:** use 4.5 Contractor Status Report when its source
  becomes available; 5.1 audits procurement evidence and supplier performance,
  and 5.2 closes the contract.

## Document-control conventions

- Filenames use the `2-38-stakeholder-management-plan.tex` pattern: the
  identifier is stable, and each standalone source is stored in its process
  group directory.
- Keep proposed implementation guidance distinct from the source-derived
  working form, its diagnostic checklist, and review/approval record.
- Blank form fields are intentionally blank. Record real owners, assessments,
  decisions, evidence, and approvals only when supplied by the project.
- Do not alter source-defined scoring scales, classifications, form labels, or
  diagnostic prompts without an approved, traceable change.
- PDFs and LaTeX auxiliary files are build outputs and are not committed.

## Compilation and publishing

The repository enforces pdfLaTeX and discovers standalone roots under `src/`.
Run the preflight and build the Architecture category with:

```bash
make preflight
make build-category-architecture
make publish    # optional: stage complete PDFs under public/pdfs/
```

`make build-category-architecture` uses the source-adjacent output model by default. `make publish` stages the full PDF corpus under `public/pdfs/architecture/governance/ccoe/`. The verified corpus feeds the Pages workflow. These are Architecture documents, not Cornell Notes; a successful build is not project approval.
