\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Change request coordination ?
\item[2.] Who is responsible to authorize changes?
\item[3.] What is a Change Request Form?
\item[4.] What are the basic mechanics of the Change Advisory Board (CAB)?
\item[5.] How can changes be graded?
\item[6.] Who will perform the change?
\item[7.] Why were your requested changes rejected or not made?
\item[8.] Can static requirements change attributes like the size of the change be used to predict reliability in execution?
\item[9.] How do team members communicate with each other?
\item[10.] When do you create a change request?
\item[11.] Are there requirements attributes that are strongly related to the complexity and size?
\item[12.] What type of changes does change control take into account?
\item[13.] How well do experienced software developers predict software change?
\item[14.] Since there are no change requests in your Cloud Center of Excellence project at this point, what must you have before you begin?
\item[15.] How are changes requested (forms, method of communication)?
\item[16.] Will all change requests be unconditionally tracked through this process?
\item[17.] Will there be a change request form in use?
\item[18.] How does your organization control changes before and after software is released to a customer?
\item[19.] How shall the implementation of changes be recorded?
\item[20.] What is the function of the change control committee?
\item[21.] What is the relationship between requirements attributes and reliability?
\item[22.] How does a team identify the discrete elements of a configuration?
\item[23.] Are there requirements attributes that can discriminate between high and low reliability?
\item[24.] What is the change request log?
\item[25.] How is the change documented (format, content, storage)?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
