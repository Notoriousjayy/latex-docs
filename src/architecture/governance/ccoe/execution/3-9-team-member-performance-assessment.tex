\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] What makes them effective?
\item[2.] Are there any safeguards to prevent intentional or unintentional rating errors?
\item[3.] Is there reluctance to join a team?
\item[4.] How should adaptive assessments be implemented?
\item[5.] Are any validation activities performed?
\item[6.] How is assessment information achieved, stored?
\item[7.] To what degree are the goals realistic?
\item[8.] How accurately is your plan implemented?
\item[9.] What is the large, desired outcome?
\item[10.] New skills/knowledge gained this year?
\item[11.] How do you work together to improve teaching and learning?
\item[12.] How do you start collaborating?
\item[13.] How do you currently use the time that is available?
\item[14.] Are the goals SMART ?
\item[15.] What variables that affect team members achievement are within your control?
\item[16.] What are the evaluation strategies (e.g., reaction, learning, behavior, results) used. What evaluation results did you have?
\item[17.] What are the basic principles and objectives of performance measurement and assessment?
\item[18.] How is performance assessment used in making future award decisions including options and extend/compete decisions?
\item[19.] Does the rater (supervisor) have the authority or responsibility to tell an employee that the employees performance is unsatisfactory?
\item[20.] Are the draft goals SMART ?
\item[21.] How is your organizations Strategic Management System tied to performance measurement?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
