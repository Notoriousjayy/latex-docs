\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] To what degree do members articulate the goals beyond the team membership?
\item[2.] How hard did you try to make a good selection?
\item[3.] To what degree are the members clear on what they are individually responsible for and what they are jointly responsible for?
\item[4.] To what degree is the team cognizant of small wins to be celebrated along the way?
\item[5.] To what degree are the goals ambitious?
\item[6.] How do you recognize and praise members for contributions?
\item[7.] To what degree are sub-teams possible or necessary?
\item[8.] Do you promptly inform members about major developments that may affect them?
\item[9.] Social categorization and intergroup behaviour: Does minimal intergroup discrimination make social identity more positive?
\item[10.] What makes opportunities more or less obvious?
\item[11.] What structural changes have you made or are you preparing to make?
\item[12.] To what degree will the team ensure that all members equitably share the work essential to the success of the team?
\item[13.] To what degree can team members meet frequently enough to accomplish the teams ends?
\item[14.] To what degree does the team possess adequate membership to achieve its ends?
\item[15.] To what degree can the team ensure that all members are individually and jointly accountable for the teams purpose, goals, approach, and work-products?
\item[16.] To what degree do team members understand one anothers roles and skills?
\item[17.] What is method variance?
\item[18.] To what degree are the skill areas critical to team performance present?
\item[19.] Can team performance be reliably measured in simulator and live exercises using the same assessment tool?
\item[20.] To what degree are the teams goals and objectives clear, simple, and measurable?
\item[21.] To what degree do team members frequently explore the teams purpose and its implications?
\item[22.] Which situations call for a more extreme type of adaptiveness in which team members actually re-define roles?
\item[23.] To what degree does the teams approach to its work allow for modification and improvement over time?
\item[24.] How hard do you try to make a good selection?
\item[25.] Can familiarity breed backup?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
