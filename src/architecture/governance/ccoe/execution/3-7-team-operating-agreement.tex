\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] Do you post any action items, due dates, and responsibilities on the team website?
\item[2.] What individual strengths does each team member bring to the group?
\item[3.] To whom do you deliver your services?
\item[4.] Has the appropriate access to relevant data and analysis capability been granted?
\item[5.] The method to be used in the decision making process; Will it be consensus, majority rule, or the supervisor having the final say?
\item[6.] Do you listen for voice tone and word choice to understand the meaning behind words?
\item[7.] What is culture?
\item[8.] Do you upload presentation materials in advance and test the technology?
\item[9.] Communication protocols: how will the team communicate?
\item[10.] What resources can be provided for the team in terms of equipment, space, time for training, protected time and space for meetings, and travel allowances?
\item[11.] Does your team need access to all documents and information at all times?
\item[12.] Do team members need to frequently communicate as a full group to make timely decisions?
\item[13.] What is group supervision?
\item[14.] Do you record meetings for the already stated unable to attend?
\item[15.] Are team roles clearly defined and accepted?
\item[16.] Methodologies: how will key team processes be implemented, such as training, research, work deliverable production, review and approval processes, knowledge management, and meeting procedures?
\item[17.] Do you use a parking lot for any items that are important and outside of the agenda?
\item[18.] What is teaming?
\item[19.] Are there the right people on your team?
\item[20.] Do you solicit member feedback about meetings and what would make them better?
\item[21.] Have you set the goals and objectives of the team?
\item[22.] How will group handle unplanned absences?
\item[23.] Did you delegate tasks such as taking meeting minutes, presenting a topic and soliciting input?
\item[24.] Conflict resolution: how will disputes and other conflicts be mediated or resolved?
\item[25.] Reimbursements: how will the team members be reimbursed for expenses and time commitments?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
