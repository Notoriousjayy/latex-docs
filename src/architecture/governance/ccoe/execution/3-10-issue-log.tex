\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] How do you manage communications?
\item[2.] Are there potential barriers between the team and the stakeholder?
\item[3.] Are stakeholder roles recognized by your organization?
\item[4.] Which team member will work with each stakeholder?
\item[5.] Do you feel more overwhelmed by stakeholders?
\item[6.] Do you have members of your team responsible for certain stakeholders?
\item[7.] What date was the issue resolved?
\item[8.] Is access to the Issue Log controlled?
\item[9.] Why do you manage human resources?
\item[10.] Who is the stakeholder?
\item[11.] What is a Stakeholder?
\item[12.] Is the issue log kept in a safe place?
\item[13.] Are they needed?
\item[14.] What is the impact on the risks?
\item[15.] What would have to change?
\item[16.] How were past initiatives successful?
\item[17.] Are the Cloud Center of Excellence project issues uniquely identified, including to which product they refer?
\item[18.] How is this initiative related to other portfolios, programs, or Cloud Center of Excellence projects?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
