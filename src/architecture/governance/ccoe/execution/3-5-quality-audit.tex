\docsection{03 \quad Source diagnostic checklist}
\textit{The following questions are preserved as diagnostic prompts from the original file, including any broad or sector-specific wording. They do not indicate that a condition has been verified. Mark N/A where not applicable.}
\begingroup\small\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.1em, itemsep=4pt]
\item[1.] For each device to be reconditioned, are device specifications, such as appropriate engineering drawings, component specifications and software specifications, maintained?
\item[2.] Is your organizational structure established and each positions responsibility defined?
\item[3.] Do the suppliers use a formal quality system?
\item[4.] Are all employees made aware of device defects which may occur from the improper performance of specific jobs?
\item[5.] Are storage areas and reconditioning operations designed to prevent mix-ups and assure orderly handling of both the distressed and reconditioned devices?
\item[6.] How does your organization know that it provides a safe and healthy environment?
\item[7.] How do you indicate the extent to which your personnel would be expected to contribute to the work effort?
\item[8.] Are training programs documented?
\item[9.] It is inappropriate to seek information about the Audit Panels preliminary views including questions like why do you ask that?
\item[10.] How does your organization know that its research planning and management systems are appropriately effective and constructive in enabling quality research outcomes?
\item[11.] Is progress against the intentions measurable?
\item[12.] Is there a written procedure for receiving materials?
\item[13.] Are complaint files maintained?
\item[14.] How does your organization know that its security arrangements are appropriately effective and constructive?
\item[15.] How does your organization know that the range and quality of its accommodation, catering and transportation services are appropriately effective and constructive?
\item[16.] Is there a risk that information provided by management may not always be reliable?
\item[17.] What experience do staff have in the type of work that the audit entails?
\item[18.] Are there appropriate means for intervening if necessary?
\item[19.] What does the organizarion look for in a Quality audit?
\item[20.] If your organization thinks it is doing something well, can it prove this?
\item[21.] How does your organization know that its system for governing staff behaviour is appropriately effective and constructive?
\item[22.] Health and safety arrangements; stress management workshops. How does your organization know that it provides a safe and healthy environment?
\item[23.] Will the evidence likely be sufficient and appropriate?
\item[24.] Why are you trying to do it?
\item[25.] Have personnel cleanliness and health requirements been established?
\end{enumerate}\endgroup
