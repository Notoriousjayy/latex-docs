\textbf{How are availability and outage duration related?} & Availability typically expresses successful service operation over an observation interval; exclusions and eligible downtime must be defined before reporting.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Calculate availability from measured eligible downtime.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{How is reliability different?} & Reliability studies failure frequency and continuity characteristics, including measures such as MTBF. A service can be available despite recurring incidents if restoration is fast.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Compare services with equal availability but different incident counts.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{Which dimensions are performance metrics?} & Capacity, request response time, completion time and startup time measure different experience or throughput constraints.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Design a workload test at representative concurrency.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{How can scalability be measured?} & Horizontal and vertical scalability track expansion while checking throughput and diminishing returns.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Plot throughput against active instance count.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{What measures resiliency?} & Switch-over and recovery durations provide evidence of disruption tolerance; claimed redundancy alone does not establish those times.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Time fault injection to restored service.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{How are SLAs made enforceable?} & Define measurement source, observation window, allowed exclusions, escalation and remedies in addition to a numerical target.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Create an SLA term sheet from measurable indicators.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\end{longtable}\normalsize
\section{Synthesis and architecture connections}
Connect architecture selection to operating commitments. Attribute each obligation to a consumer or provider, describe its measurement source and produce traceable evidence for risk, cost and service quality.
\begin{workbox}[title={Applied deliverable}]
Translate service needs into measurable objectives and SLA evidence.\\[4pt]Create a one-page architecture or operations artifact with: (1) assumptions, (2) a decision and alternatives, (3) control ownership, (4) observable acceptance criteria, and (5) a failed-case test.
\end{workbox}
