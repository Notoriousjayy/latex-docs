\textbf{What is remote administration?} & It enables approved configuration and operational actions at a distance. Privileged access must be gated, logged and revocable.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Design an emergency access path with expiry.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{What does resource management control?} & It coordinates lifecycle, allocation, configuration and administrative policy for cloud IT resources.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Specify desired-state drift and corrective behavior.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{How does SLA management differ from monitoring?} & Monitors produce evidence; management processes maintain commitments, negotiate breaches and govern service expectations.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Simulate a breach review from measurement to resolution.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{How does billing management relate to usage?} & Metered units need attribution, pricing policy and reconciliation to create defensible invoices or chargeback reports.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Build a reconciliation worksheet.}\\[8pt]\midrule
\textbf{What are management-plane failure risks?} & Administrative permissions and automations can create large blast radii. Segregation of duties and change records mitigate these risks.\\[3pt]\textcolor{petrol}{\emph{Practice:} Identify a dangerous combination of permissions and its compensating check.}\\[8pt]\midrule

\section{Synthesis and architecture connections}
Treat each mechanism as a deployable capability, not a complete architecture. Identify the infrastructure prerequisite, management-plane owner, dependent controls and the negative/failure evidence expected from an implementation.
\begin{workbox}[title={Applied deliverable}]
Separate governance, administration, service assurance and charging functions.\\[4pt]Create a one-page architecture or operations artifact with: (1) assumptions, (2) a decision and alternatives, (3) control ownership, (4) observable acceptance criteria, and (5) a failed-case test.
\end{workbox}
