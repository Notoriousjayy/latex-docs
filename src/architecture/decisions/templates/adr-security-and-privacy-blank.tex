\section{Domain-specific decision inputs}
Complete the following prompts with referenceable designs, measurements,
threat assessments or operational constraints; mark inapplicable prompts
explicitly rather than removing necessary decision context.
\begin{itemize}
\item Protected assets, classifications, data flows, trust boundaries, privileged users and abuse cases.
\item Adversary capabilities, plausible misuse paths, attack surface, detection assumptions and prioritized threats.
\item Authentication, authorization, tenancy isolation, secrets, key lifecycle and software supply-chain controls.
\item Privacy purpose, minimization, retention, transfer restrictions, consent/legal basis (where applicable) and deletion behavior.
\item Exceptions, compensating controls, residual risk acceptance and accountable authority.
\end{itemize}

\section{Domain-specific validation and review gates}
The proposed decision is ready for acceptance only after its applicable
tests have objective pass/fail thresholds, an owner and dated evidence.
\begin{itemize}
\item Threat-model review and proof of control enforcement at each trust boundary.
\item Negative authorization tests, secret-rotation exercises, encryption/key-access evidence and logging checks.
\item Security testing with documented scope, remediation owner, residual risk sign-off and privacy review.
\end{itemize}
