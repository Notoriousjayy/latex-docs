\section{Domain-specific decision inputs}
Complete the following prompts with referenceable designs, measurements,
threat assessments or operational constraints; mark inapplicable prompts
explicitly rather than removing necessary decision context.
\begin{itemize}
\item Allowed business tasks, user stakes, model/provider selection and documented non-goals.
\item Data provenance, prompt/RAG inputs, residency, retention, evaluation datasets, licensing and sensitive-data controls.
\item Agent tool permissions, sandbox boundaries, human approval points, untrusted content and prompt-injection threats.
\item Quality metrics, adversarial/edge cases, fairness and task-specific failure severity; human fallback strategy.
\item Model/version drift, operational monitoring, token/compute budgets, provider dependence and shutdown controls.
\end{itemize}

\section{Domain-specific validation and review gates}
The proposed decision is ready for acceptance only after its applicable
tests have objective pass/fail thresholds, an owner and dated evidence.
\begin{itemize}
\item Offline evaluation on versioned datasets with documented acceptance thresholds and known failures.
\item Red-team/abuse tests for prompt injection, exfiltration, tool misuse and unauthorized actions.
\item Human approval and kill-switch rehearsals, rollback/fallback tests and post-deployment drift monitoring.
\end{itemize}
