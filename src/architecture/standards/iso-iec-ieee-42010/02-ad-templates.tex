\section{How to instantiate this template}
Duplicate the structure for a particular EoI. Replace all \texttt{[TODO]} values; remove illustrative entries or explicitly label them as non-operative examples. Maintain unique identifiers and stable cross-references. A record can reside in a repository or tool rather than in this document, provided the AD includes or references it where required.\par
\begin{Guidance}Use one manifest as the AD's entry point. Keep identifiers stable across versions, for example \texttt{SH-*} (stakeholders), \texttt{CN-*} (concerns), \texttt{SP-*} (perspectives), \texttt{AS-*} (aspects), \texttt{VP-*} (viewpoints), \texttt{VW-*} (views), \texttt{VC-*} (view components), \texttt{CO-*} (correspondences), \texttt{CM-*} (correspondence methods) and \texttt{ADR-*} (decisions). Identifier schemes are local conventions.\end{Guidance}
