\section{Audit method and reporting limitations}
\begin{Requirement}\FlagReq{} The standard's five conformance cases concern the \emph{specifications} of: AD (Clause 6), ADF (7.1), ADL (7.2), architecture viewpoint (8.1) and model kind (8.2). For a claim, demonstrate that the relevant requirements are met; do not tailor away required provisions. \Source{4}\end{Requirement}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{p{0.20\linewidth}X}\toprule\textbf{Outcome}&\textbf{Interpretation for this suite}\\\midrule
\textbf{PASS}&The artifact and evidence demonstrate the provision for the claimed scope.\\
\textbf{FAIL}&The provision is not met, or the documented evidence contradicts it.\\
\textbf{OPEN}&Evidence absent, ambiguous or awaiting independent review; cannot count as pass.\\
\textbf{N/A}&Only where the original conditional provision truly does not apply; record reasoning. Not a license to tailor mandatory clauses.\\
\textbf{REC}&Recommended (\emph{should}) rather than a mandatory conformance criterion.\\
\textbf{LOCAL}&Optional practice added by this suite or an organization's own governance.\\\bottomrule\end{tabularx}

\begin{Requirement}\textbf{Critical distinction.} A conformance claim concerns the stated class of \emph{architecture-description artifact}. It does not certify the security, safety, performance, legal compliance or operational correctness of the EoI. A checklist completed without sufficient evidence cannot itself establish conformance. \Source{1 and 4}\end{Requirement}
