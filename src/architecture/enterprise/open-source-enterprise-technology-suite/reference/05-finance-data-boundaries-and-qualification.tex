\section{Mandatory business accounting requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item Determine required legal entities, chart of accounts, double-entry enforcement, approval segregation and immutable journal audit trails.
\item Assess tax, sales tax/VAT, payroll, AP/AR, invoice lifecycle, inventory, bank reconciliation and month-end period close.
\item Document accounting standard, local jurisdiction and accountant sign-off before production designation.
\item No automatic full transaction export into Eramba, SuiteCRM, iTop, observability, or general data warehouses.
\item Prefer controlled desktop deployment and encrypted, restored backups when multi-user access is not necessary.
\end{itemize}
\section{Privacy and sync controls}
\begin{itemize}
\item Pin import formats and validate provenance; quarantine financial CSV/OFX/QIF and watch for spreadsheet formula injection in exports.
\item Differentiate Actual Budget optional budget E2EE from unencrypted/other-protected bank-sync credentials and keys.
\item Record brokers, market data subscriptions, import OAuth permissions, backup locations and key custody.
\item Use explicit owner consent for any aggregated finance telemetry; exclude merchant, account, beneficiary and transaction descriptions.
\end{itemize}
\section{Finance architecture gate}
Finance subplatform receives a separate deployment threat model, identity context, retention regime and recovery plan. Finance transactions cannot share security findings queues, common test databases or ordinary developer logs.\par
\textbf{Decision / gate.} A budget/portfolio tracker cannot be labeled an accounting or compliance system without independently demonstrated controls.\par
