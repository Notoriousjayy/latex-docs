\vspace{9pt}{\LARGE\bfseries\color{navy} Architecture Decision: Segregate personal finance systems from enterprise operations}\par
\vspace{8pt}{\small\color{muted} ADR-0003-FINANCE-SEGREGATION | Revision 1.0 | 31 October 2026}\par
\end{center}
\vspace{8pt}\hrule\vspace{12pt}
\textbf{Purpose and status.} Proposed decision pending an architecture board decision; no implicit production authorization.\par
\textbf{Evidence status:} Proposed / requires local validation.\par
\section{Decision status}
Accepted as proposal; architecture board sign-off, date, owner and ticket are not supplied.\par
\section{Context}
Finance tools serve personal, budgeting and investment functions with high privacy impact.\par
\section{Decision}
Keep finance isolated, use manual approved aggregates, and qualify any business ledger separately.\par
\section{Considered alternatives}
Integrate all applications directly; or deploy fully approved accounting/ERP with enterprise controls.\par
\section{Consequences and tests}
Blocks default replication of raw transaction data.\par
\section{Review triggers}
\begin{itemize}
\item Change to tool edition/license or enterprise source-of-truth policy.
\item Material security incident, failed proof of concept, or retirement of a supported API.
\item Regulatory, financial accounting or privacy requirements change.
\end{itemize}
