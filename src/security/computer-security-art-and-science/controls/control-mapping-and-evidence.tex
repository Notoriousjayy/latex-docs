\section{Mapping methodology}
All cross-framework relationships are \textbf{candidate thematic mappings}, not official crosswalk assertions. The machine-readable \texttt{control-crosswalk.csv} is the suite-local register. Verify exact requirement IDs, standard edition and applicability in the official documents before policy adoption.
\section{Security claim trace}
Use the chain \emph{book topic $\rightarrow$ objective $\rightarrow$ design requirement $\rightarrow$ implementation $\rightarrow$ verification $\rightarrow$ evidence $\rightarrow$ exception/approval}. Split a control into smaller claims when one verification method cannot validate all its mechanisms.

\section{Assessment rules}
Reject empty evidence pointers, ambiguous ownership or claims of implemented controls based only on a documented design. Reassess on material changes and retain specific citations to exact control IDs in a versioned official publication. NIST CSF is an outcome framework; ATT\&CK is an adversary behavior knowledge base; ISO certification is a separate process.
