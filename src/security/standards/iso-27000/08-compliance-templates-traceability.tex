\section{Statement of Applicability completion rules}
\begin{enumerate}
\item List relevant Annex A control references and organization-defined necessary controls not represented there.
\item Record whether each is determined necessary for treatment and state context-sensitive inclusion/exclusion reasoning.
\item Record implementation state distinctly from applicability; an included control may remain unimplemented.
\item Relate included controls to risk scenarios or other applicable information security requirements.
\item For excluded Annex A controls, include defensible justifications; avoid vague labels such as N/A without reasoning.
\item Version and approve the SoA through controlled documentation; re-evaluate after material changes.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Corrective action and closure record}
\begin{itemize}
\item Finding ID, precise failed criteria and source of objective evidence.
\item Immediate containment and documented corrections; include scope and consequences.
\item Root-cause analysis with evaluation of similar occurrences.
\item Preventive system-level change and named implementing owner.
\item Reperformance plan, tester independence, observed result and closure approval.
\end{itemize}

\section{Versioning and data-quality conventions}
\begin{itemize}
\item Use durable IDs: REQ-27001-6.1.3, CTL-8.8, R-APP-014, TC-8.8-01, EVD-2026-XXXX.
\item Use ISO 8601 dates, UTC timestamps for machine evidence and clear timezone for human events.
\item Restrict evidence by sensitivity; never paste live secrets, personal details or raw customer data into templates.
\item Record tested population, incomplete sources, inability to access systems and evidence gaps transparently.
\item Preserve all approvals and modifications with audit history rather than overwriting previous versions.
\end{itemize}

\clearpage\section*{Source and interpretation policy}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Source and interpretation policy}
\textbf{Source editions:} ISO/IEC 27000:2018 (overview and vocabulary); ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (requirements and normative Annex A); ISO/IEC 27002:2022 corrected 2022-03 (control guidance). Specific clause identifiers are references to the supplied editions, not reproduced control text.\par
\textbf{Interpretation:} ``Source-grounded paraphrase'' describes a concept in the cited source. ``Engineering recommendation'' is a practical model, technology choice, owner, evidence example, frequency, template or scoring convention authored for this suite; it is not a claim that ISO prescribes that exact approach.\par
\textbf{Conformity:} Clauses 4--10 of ISO/IEC 27001 govern ISMS conformity. Applicability of controls is determined through risk treatment, with Annex A used as a completeness cross-check and exclusions justified in the Statement of Applicability (SoA). Source documents alone neither certify an ISMS nor prove control operating effectiveness.\par
\textbf{Source licensing:} Do not distribute or embed the licensed standard PDFs in repositories or distribute copied normative text. Review the editions, amendments, and certification scheme applicable to each assessment before relying on this work.\par
