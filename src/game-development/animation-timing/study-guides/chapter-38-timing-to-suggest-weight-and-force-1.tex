\section{Engineering translation (supplementary)}
\textbf{Artifact:} mass cue timing comparison. Record fps as a rational value, duration in seconds, frame boundaries, and whether transforms are evaluated from elapsed time or counted exposures.
\textbf{Exercise:} Make identical shapes read as light versus heavy. Prepare a baseline, one controlled timing variation, and a comparison. Keep pose endpoints and camera setup fixed unless the exercise is specifically about those variables.
