\section{Provenance and purpose}
\textbf{Source crosswalk:} Chapters 11--16, 31--33, 68--71. This authoring form is supplementary and independently developed.
\section{How to complete}
Plan a 48-display-frame shot with at least one hold, one action burst, and one dialogue cue. Mark all 48 frame rows and verify layer coverage. Record the observed evidence, measurement environment, evaluator, and unresolved issues. 
\section{Illustrative entry}
Example: a drawing held for three display frames occupies indices 8, 9 and 10; the next drawing begins at index 11.
