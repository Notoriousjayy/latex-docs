# Source provenance and editorial boundary

**Anchor text:** Harold Whitaker, John Halas, and Tom Sito, *Timing for Animation*, 40th Anniversary Edition, third edition (2020).

The EPUB table of contents was inspected directly. The 82 chapter titles and their order were taken from the EPUB, not from a secondary list. The guides are **independently written study aids** keyed to those chapters. Their diagnostic questions, practical assignments, competency criteria and scoring are original; none reproduces the book's figures, illustration plates, exercise prompts, or significant passage text.

The book covers historical and digital animation-production methods and includes chapters explicitly devoted to interactive games. It **does not provide the executable code, numerical invariants, software architecture standards, engine API prescriptions, or testing procedures here**. Those are supplementary engineering practices, identified as such throughout the suite. Historical production conventions should not be confused with runtime engine requirements.

Recommended source notes: in a completed learning record, include chapter/page or EPUB location and your own observation of the author's image example, rather than importing copyrighted assets. The original EPUB is **not included** in this repository-ready package.
