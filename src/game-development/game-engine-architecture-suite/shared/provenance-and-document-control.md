# Document provenance and classification

Source: Jason Gregory, *Game Engine Architecture*, Third Edition, CRC Press (2018), chapters 1–17.

The study guides follow the official chapter sequence and section numbers. Original explanation, testing, lab code, templates, and workflow guidance are authored specifically for this curriculum. The documents are *not* transcriptions of the text; consult the source for its complete argument, figures, and proprietary engine examples.

Modernization boundary: Python 3, C++20, CMake, sanitizers, ECS teaching examples, CSV timing, vulnerability checks, and generalized CI practices are *supplementary implementation exercises*. Do not ascribe these precise API choices to the 2018 textbook.

Printing rule: `*-template.tex` may contain directions and illustrative guidance; `*-blank.tex` must be physically usable in print and must contain no bracketed placeholders or prefilled responses.

Architecture taxonomy: the four architecture specifications are views/descriptions of the whole design, while `engine-architecture-decision-record` is a decision record for a *single* choice, not an architecture-description replacement.
