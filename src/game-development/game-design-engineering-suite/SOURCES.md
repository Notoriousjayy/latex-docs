# Source and provenance register

## Primary source

Schell, Jesse. *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses*. Third edition. CRC Press / Taylor & Francis Group, copyright 2020, ISBN (paperback) 978-1-138-63205-9. Uploaded 653-page PDF used for source-page navigation and chapter/lens index identification.

- Book chapter titles and their printed start pages: chapter-to-artifact-crosswalk.csv.
- Book lens names and their printed page locators: lens-to-chapter-index.csv.
- Study guides: original explanatory prompts, objectives, activities and original technical extensions, not substitutions for full chapter reading.
- Thematic grouping: seven learning phases constructed for engineering study. This is not represented as a source-prescribed curriculum.
- Prototype tests, metrics, source code, formal models, privacy protocols, architecture patterns, scoring and quality gates: supplementary engineering practices, not the author's statements or endorsed tooling.

## Publication and copyright boundaries

Do not distribute the uploaded book as part of the suite. Consult the book itself for full lens questions, figures, narrative examples, or any textbook exercise. The crosswalk preserves brief titles/page locators only. References to the book are bibliographic, not licenses to redistribute it.
