\section{Use, assumptions, and source boundary}
These exercises are original engineering practice inspired by Schell's chapters on mechanics, balance, prototypes, playtesting, and documentation. The workbook is not a reproduction of textbook exercises; it assumes software engineering proficiency and asks learners to state assumptions explicitly.


\section{Record of work}
For every exercise submit (1) a hypothesis, (2) assumptions and definitions, (3) workings or code, (4) negative cases, (5) decision and uncertainty, and (6) traceability identifiers.


\section{Formalize the state}
Define S with current health in 0..10, energy in 0..5, and a turn flag. Enumerate at least three legal actions and five illegal actions.


\subsection{Required artifacts}
State schema; transition preconditions; four invariants; transition table.
