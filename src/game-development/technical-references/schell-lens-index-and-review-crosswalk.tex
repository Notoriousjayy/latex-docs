\section{Review protocol}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Specify the uncertain design decision and player audience.
\item Select two to five lenses, read their original passages, and explain why these lenses are relevant.
\item Record claims from the author separately from your own hypothesis.
\item Create a small test and collect player evidence.
\item Revise the design, documenting conflicts between competing perspectives.
\end{enumerate}
