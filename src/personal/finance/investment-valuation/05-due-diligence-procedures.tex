\front{Investment Due Diligence}{Evidence, Underwriting and Decision SOP}{Ten-stage analyst process with challenge gates, checklists, templates and governance}

\section{Purpose, gate policy, and standard file package}
\src\ Graham--Dodd's emphasis on sources, accounting quality, claims, operating resilience and supervision motivates a disciplined evidence process. \ext\ This standard operating procedure translates that idea into an auditable, repeatable investment workflow for listed equities, bonds, convertibles, and private claims.

A complete file contains a cover sheet, security term summary, source registry, normalized financials, claim waterfall, valuation workbook, thesis memorandum, independent dissent review, committee decision, order/execution record, and watchlist plan.

\section{Stage 0: Mandate and conflict screening}
\textbf{Inputs:} prospectus/mandate, investment universe, restrictions, concentration limits, regulatory obligations, affiliate and insider restrictions. \textbf{Tasks:} confirm instrument eligibility, information-barrier and MNPI policy, restricted lists, beneficial ownership and conflicts. \textbf{Output:} eligibility/decline decision. \textbf{Stop gate:} prohibition, unresolved legal question, or material conflict without authorized disposition.

\section{Stage 1: Identify the exact instrument and counterparties}
Capture CUSIP/ISIN when applicable, legal obligor, guarantees, underlying reference security, shares outstanding, debt covenants, maturity, currency, exchange/listing, and settlement terms. Draw the ownership/creditor stack. \textbf{Stop gate:} claim rank or counterparty cannot be independently identified.

\section{Stage 2: Evidence acquisition and freshness}
Prioritize regulatory disclosures, audited statements and notes, contracts, material-event disclosures, regulator notices, and independent industry evidence. Use investor presentations as explanatory rather than sole proof. Store retrieval URLs, document hashes where lawful, reporting dates, filing dates, and transformations.

\section{Stage 3: Business economics and management}
Analyze addressable market, customer concentration, product substitution, pricing power, unit contribution, working capital, supply chain, reinvestment requirements, governance, capital allocation, incentives, past related-party transactions and auditor changes. Do not infer competitive advantage from growth alone. \textbf{Deliverable:} 2-page business model and fragility analysis with disconfirming evidence.

\section{Stage 4: Accounting and cash-flow normalization}
Reconcile revenue to cash collections, identify accrual quality, normalize clearly nonrecurring items, examine depreciation assumptions, impairments, lease obligations, capitalized software and acquisitions, share-based compensation, minority interests, and contingencies. Compute cash conversion, ROIC, and maintenance cash generation. \textbf{Stop gate:} unexplained cash--earnings divergence or material unreconciled statements.

\section{Stage 5: Debt terms, covenants and legal claim rank}
Summarize security interests, legal entity, structural subordination, maturity ladder, rate resets, call provisions, covenants and amendment rights. Produce base and stressed covenant headroom. Explicitly record missing contracts and restricted-payment baskets. \textbf{Stop gate:} cannot determine priority/recovery exposure.

\section{Stage 6: Multi-method valuation}
Generate at least two independent anchors suitable to the instrument (e.g., normalized earnings + DCF, asset realization + comparable transactions, fixed-income cash flows + recovery, segment sum-of-parts + downside liquidation). For each, report assumptions, bear/base/bull range, evidence strength, adverse event, exit liquidity and discount to price.

\section{Stage 7: Permanent-loss and sensitivity assessment}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Describe the exact path to insolvency or loss of seniority, including refinancing deadlines.
\item Quantify operating downturn, margin contraction, FX, rates, collateral realization and governance shocks.
\item Separate model uncertainty from irreducible uncertainty (litigation, regulation, macro shocks).
\item Compute loss severity, capital at risk, position liquidity, and exit constraints.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Stage 8: Independent challenge and investment committee}
Assign a reviewer not responsible for the initial thesis. Reviewer reproduces core metrics and writes a dissent memo with the strongest plausible countercase. Committee packet includes a decision log: thesis, price limit, sizing, exposures, covenants, catalysts, thesis invalidation criteria and next review.

\section{Stage 9: Execution and post-investment supervision}
Confirm liquidity, market impact, settlement risks and order limits. Monitor earnings and filings, restatements, debt maturities, ratings, covenant tests, issuer cash balances, share issuance, governance, competing risks and price--value changes. Re-underwrite on new material information; avoid treating losses as proof of cheapness.

\section{Formal evidence checklist}
\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] Legal security and beneficial claim identity independently verified.
\item[$\square$] Primary reporting sources and cash-flow reconciliation attached.
\item[$\square$] Auditor reservations, restatements, contingencies and litigation reviewed.
\item[$\square$] Related parties, ownership and control examined.
\item[$\square$] Financial metrics computed at both issuer and legal-entity levels where necessary.
\item[$\square$] All valuation scenarios tied to market and balance-sheet definitions.
\item[$\square$] Fixed-income maturity and covenant sensitivity prepared where applicable.
\item[$\square$] Permanent capital loss pathways and red flags documented.
\item[$\square$] Independent review, approval, order controls and watchlist assigned.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reusable investment thesis memorandum}
\begin{methodbox}
\textbf{Header:} instrument, date, author, reviewer, currency, position-limit requested.\\
\textbf{Core decision:} price, value range, margin of safety, expected time horizon.\\
\textbf{Evidence:} 3 favorable facts, 3 adverse facts, 3 material unknowns.\\
\textbf{Loss thesis:} solvency, governance, collateral, liquidation and liquidity risks.\\
\textbf{Catalysts:} events with evidence and estimated timing; absence of catalyst stated.\\
\textbf{Monitoring:} metrics, alerts, sources and explicit thesis-invalidation triggers.
\end{methodbox}

\section{Exception, escalation and audit procedure}
Maintain an issue register with identifier, missing evidence, materiality, owner, deadline, remedial step and closure proof. Unresolved material issues mean \emph{defer} or \emph{reject}, not assumed remediation. Approvals are limited by mandate, investment committee authority, and legal/compliance policy; record authorized exceptions with expiry and independent sign-off.
