\section{Classical claims and new claims}
The book supplies descriptions and historical research references. A present-day implementation's performance, memory safety, parser diagnostics, Unicode behavior or security guarantees must be established independently; do not attribute them automatically to a classic paper.\PTSource{Chapter 18 and the book's prefaces.}
\section{Reading register fields}
\begin{enumerate}\item Full bibliographic identity and reproducible stable locator.\item Model: grammar class, input assumptions, recognition versus forest/tree output.\item Main contribution: theorem, analysis, algorithm, implementation or benchmark.\item Proof obligations, asymptotic work/space and omitted constants.\item Evidence quality: examples, datasets, code, replicability and peer review.\item Limitations, counterexamples and relevance to the selected project.\end{enumerate}
\section{Citation hygiene}
Do not infer a feature from a paper title. Distinguish the year of the original result from a new tool's version. Cite the primary result for theoretical claims and the current implementation documentation for tool behavior. Avoid reproducing published figures or algorithms verbatim in the library.
