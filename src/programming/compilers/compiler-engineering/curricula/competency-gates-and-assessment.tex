\section{Gate requirements}
\begin{itemize}
\item G1: At least level 2 for lexical/parsing correctness before typed lowering.
\item G2: At least level 2 in IR and CFG analysis before any global optimization.
\item G3: At least level 3 in analyses and optimization before target-code performance claims.
\item G4: At least level 3 in backend and security before capstone sign-off.
\end{itemize}

Use the \texttt{compiler-capstone-evidence-template.tex} and paired blank. Archive only public/harmless test inputs; never paste secrets or untrusted binaries into documents. Link source revision, reproducible command, toolchain version, observed result, review sign-off and known limitations.
