\section{Guided technical analysis}
\subsection{Error statements have quantifiers}
The expression $f(n)=g(n)+O(h(n))$ means $|f(n)-g(n)|\le Ch(n)$ eventually for a constant $C$ and positive $h$. It is weaker than $f(n)\sim g(n)$ when $h$ is not smaller order than $g$.
\subsection{Harmonic numbers}
The approximation $H_n=\log n+\gamma+1/(2n)+O(n^{-2})$ refines the coarser $H_n=\log n+O(1)$. For random BST expected internal path length this gives
\[
2(n+1)H_n-4n=2n\log n+(2\gamma-4)n+2\log n+O(1).
\]
\subsection{Sums versus integrals}
The integral test estimates $\sum_{k=1}^n k^p$ by $n^{p+1}/(p+1)$ for $p>-1$. Euler--Maclaurin can yield correction terms, but endpoints and derivative conditions matter. When applying Laplace's method, identify the dominating interior or boundary point, the smoothness of the phase, and whether the maximum is nondegenerate.
