\section{Claim classification}
Determine whether the result is exact, an inequality, a big-$O$ relation, a little-$o$ relation, or asymptotic equivalence. Record every limit and range of validity.
\section{Worked calibration}
$H_n=\log n+\gamma+O(1/n)$ implies $H_n/\log n\to1$. The converse does not identify the Euler--Mascheroni constant or the $1/n$ correction.
