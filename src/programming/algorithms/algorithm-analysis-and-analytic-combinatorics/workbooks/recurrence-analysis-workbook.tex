\section{Model and operation unit}
A recurrence is a mathematical claim about an operation count, not just a convenient algebraic expression. Distinguish exact and asymptotic relations, and record the base domain.
\section{Worked calibration: a telescoping difference}
If $d_0=0$ and $d_n=d_{n-1}+3n-2$, sum over $n$ to obtain $d_n=3n(n+1)/2-2n=(3n^2-n)/2$. Reinsert $d_{n-1}$ to check the difference.
