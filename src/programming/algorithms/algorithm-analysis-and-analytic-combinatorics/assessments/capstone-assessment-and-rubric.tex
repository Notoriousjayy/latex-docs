\section{Capstone: falsifiable performance engineering}
\textbf{Scenario.} A library must choose one of two search/sort structures for a workload with uncertain input distribution. Choose quicksort variants, BST shape construction, or trie versus chaining. A hypothetical source of inputs is acceptable; never imply access to production data without evidence.
\section{Required deliverables}
\begin{enumerate}
\item An analysis record specifying the operation unit, input laws and two competing hypotheses.
\item A proof annex with exact recurrence or generating-function relation, small-$n$ verification, and an asymptotic statement with its domain.
\item At least one independent implementation in Python and one in C++ where equivalent semantics are possible; otherwise document why parity is not meaningful.
\item A benchmark manifest and raw data with no discarded failed observations.
\item A comparative results table including operation count, median time, normalized cost and failure cases.
\item A structured recommendation that distinguishes model-based certainty from hardware-specific observations.
\end{enumerate}
\section{Acceptance tests}
Correctness on edge cases; deterministic seeds; command-line scripts run without manual editing; the theory-to-count discrepancy is explicitly explained; no speedup claim lacking raw evidence; at least one model violation analyzed.
\section{Scoring rubric}
\begin{longtable}{p{0.44\linewidth}p{0.17\linewidth}p{0.27\linewidth}}\toprule Criterion & Points & Demonstration \\ \midrule
Formal mathematics & 25 & Derivation, conditions, counterexamples \\
Implementation and tests & 20 & Repeatability, oracles, unit tests \\
Experiment controls & 20 & Inputs, seeds, environment, replication \\
Inference and limitations & 20 & Explain residuals, not only charts \\
Communication and provenance & 15 & Source links, evidence table, decision \\
\bottomrule\end{longtable}
\section{Review procedure}
Two reviewers independently score the capstone-review blank form; compare discrepancies of two or more scale points, request supporting evidence, then issue a consolidated disposition. This is an internal educational process, not a textbook or university credential.
