\section{Rubric and completion guidance}
Rate each dimension on a scale of 0 (no evidence) to 4 (independent, reproducible and defensible). Record concrete references to proof, tests and raw data. Total maximum is 24. Thresholds require agreement before assessment; a passing total never overrides a correctness-critical failure.
