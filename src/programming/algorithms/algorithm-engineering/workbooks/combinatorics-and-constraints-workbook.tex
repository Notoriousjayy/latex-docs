\section{Worked model: exact cover}
Let primary items be $\{A,B,C,D\}$ and options $\{A,B\}$, $\{C,D\}$, $\{A,C\}$ and $\{B,D\}$. Two valid covers exist: the first two options, or the last two. Selecting an option excludes any other option sharing one of its already-covered primary items.
\section{Worked model: SAT encoding}
For exclusive choice of Boolean variables $x,y$, use $(x\lor y)\land(\neg x\lor\neg y)$. Its satisfying assignments are precisely $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Verify with a four-row truth table. For larger exactly-one constraints, note that different encodings have different auxiliary variable and clause costs.

\section{Workbook protocol}
Write assumptions before derivations. Distinguish claims to prove, counterexamples to find and empirical questions to test. Use separate paper if a proof exceeds the available lines. Each response should end with a testable implication or explicit limitation.
