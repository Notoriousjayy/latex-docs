\section{Instructions for reuse}

\Provenance
\begin{groundedbox}The source book's Part III includes sample usability evaluation material (appendixes A--E). The templates below are new and designed for modern development and governance; they are \textbf{not transcriptions} of the source templates.\end{groundedbox}
Copy a section into a project issue, ADR repository or design review. Replace bracketed placeholders before approval. Maintain source-pattern identifiers from the companion catalog (DOS-02).

\section{T1 — Modern pattern decision card}
\begin{notebox}
\textbf{Pattern ID/title:} [e.g., H10 Clear Forms]\\
\textbf{User task and context:} [who, why, capability, device, constraints]\\
\textbf{Recurring problem and forces:} [user need; business pressure; trade-offs]\\
\textbf{Proposed modern response:} [semantic design and system responsibilities]\\
\textbf{Alternatives and consequences:} [minimum two options, including simpler one]\\
\textbf{Security/privacy/accessibility impact:} [threat, data, WCAG reference]\\
\textbf{Acceptance tests:} [happy path, negative case, AT case]\\
\textbf{Operational indicator:} [measure, sample, target, observation window]\\
\textbf{Decision / owner / review date:} [Adopt, Adapt, Replace, Retire]\par\end{notebox}
\textit{Implementation note: retain links to objective evidence and never place production credentials in review documents.}
\section{T2 — Architecture decision record (ADR)}
\begin{notebox}
\textbf{ADR:} [ID and title]\\
\textbf{Status:} [Proposed/Accepted/Superseded]\\
\textbf{Context:} [business capability, patterns, problem, measurable constraints]\\
\textbf{Decision:} [architecture option and scope]\\
\textbf{Alternatives:} [option / benefits / cost / rejection reason]\\
\textbf{Consequences:} [deployment, coupling, testing, on-call, trust boundaries]\\
\textbf{Evidence:} [experiment, workload, latency, user study]\\
\textbf{Revision trigger:} [future condition for reconsideration]\\
\textbf{Approvers:} [engineering, product, security if required]\par\end{notebox}
\textit{Implementation note: retain links to objective evidence and never place production credentials in review documents.}
\section{T3 — Research study and consent checklist}
\begin{notebox}
\textbf{Research goal:} [question not assumption]\\
\textbf{Participant inclusion:} [roles, devices, languages, disabilities relevant to task]\\
\textbf{Task script:} [neutral description and objective success event]\\
\textbf{Consent:} [voluntary; recording choice; purpose; retention; withdrawal path]\\
\textbf{Collection minimization:} [no production secrets; identifiers separated]\\
\textbf{Observation worksheet:} [completion, time, errors, verbalized confusion]\\
\textbf{Researcher inference:} [separate from observed fact]\\
\textbf{Follow-up / owner:} [finding, priority, pattern ID, decision]\par\end{notebox}
\textit{Implementation note: retain links to objective evidence and never place production credentials in review documents.}
