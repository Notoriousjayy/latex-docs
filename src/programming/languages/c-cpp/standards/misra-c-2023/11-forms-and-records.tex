\section{Release approval}
\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] All scope inclusions and supplier limitations are explicitly recorded.
\item[$\square$] Guideline enforcement outcomes reconcile to the source inventory.
\item[$\square$] No approved deviations are associated with Mandatory entries.
\item[$\square$] Required violations are corrected or fully supported by permitted deviations.
\item[$\square$] Re-categorization, if any, was considered when applying enforcement gates.
\item[$\square$] Final approval and evidence retention meet project policy.
\end{itemize}
