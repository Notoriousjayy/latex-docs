\section{Mission and deliverable architecture}
This suite provides engineering methods for adopting, implementing, teaching, and assessing the April 2023 second revision of the third edition of MISRA C. The package separates primary-source facts (identifier, category, edition applicability, analysis metadata) from newly authored interpretations, examples, checklists, and operational templates.

\section{Foundational interpretation}
\subsection{Applicable language editions}
The uploaded revision identifies C90, C99 and C11 as its language-edition applicability tags; the same C11 requirements apply to C18. A C11-only entry is not automatically applicable to C99 or C90. There is no claim that the uploaded revision provides general C23 coverage. Projects must select and document an actual language profile, target ABI, compiler and library implementation.
\subsection{Rules, directives and attributes}
A \emph{rule} has enough information in its definition to check source-level conformance, subject to analysis limitations. A \emph{directive} requires additional project context (requirements, design, behavior or review evidence). A rule's decidability and required analysis scope describe theoretical properties, not the completeness of any commercial or open-source tool.
\begin{keybox}\textbf{Mandatory}: deviations are not permitted. \textbf{Required}: noncompliance needs formal deviations. \textbf{Advisory}: follow where reasonably practical and document exceptions or other arrangements when not following them. Organizations may strengthen categories through a controlled re-categorization plan.\end{keybox}

\section{Project definition of done}
An evidence-based delivery contains a frozen build manifest, traceable requirements, source inventory, tool qualification/coverage evidence, controlled checker configuration, complete diagnostic disposition, deviation records, a guideline compliance summary, supplier status, and approval of residual risks. A successful CI build or an empty alert list is not a claim of full MISRA compliance.
\section{Recommended reading path}
Start with Technical Reference 08, then study catalog 01 and examples 02. Engineers implementing enforcement should work through 03, 04, 05 and 06. Learners follow 07 in prerequisite order, with 09 for spaced recall and 10 for practical verification. Use 11 as the dossier starting point.
