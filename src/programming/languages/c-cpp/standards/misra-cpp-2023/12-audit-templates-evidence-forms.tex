\section*{How to read normative status}\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to read normative status}
\textbf{Normative source:} the licensed MISRA C++:2023 guideline text, including any applicable \emph{Amplification} and \emph{Exception}. A guideline \emph{headline} alone is not a complete compliance test. The source's Section~3 defines classifications, categories, decidability, analysis scope, and applicability.\par
\textbf{Independent guidance:} all review questions, programming patterns, lifecycle processes, sample thresholds, ownership assignments, controls, and CI configurations in this suite are \emph{engineering recommendations}, not statements issued by MISRA.\par
\textbf{Critical limitation:} no individual example is asserted compliant with the whole standard. For formal compliance claims, follow the applicable edition of \emph{MISRA Compliance} as the source itself instructs, with a documented compliance plan and supporting evidence.\par

\section{Traceability design}
Use the source guideline identifier as a durable primary key. A rule ID joins the project applicability register to analyzer check mappings, active findings, accepted source exceptions, formal deviations, test results and release evidence. The rule catalog CSV is stable reference metadata, not a compliance register by itself.

Suggested relational keys: guideline\_id, product\_variant\_id, source\_revision, finding\_id, disposition\_id, evidence\_id, reviewer\_id and release\_id. Model cardinality explicitly: one guideline can have many findings, and one deviation may cover multiple exact sites only when its approved scope names them.


\section{Interview questions for an audit}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Show how all 179 source IDs are covered by analyzer mapping or substantiated review.
\item Show that tool-executed build variants match shipped configurations.
\item Trace Rule 6.8.2 from source page to a finding, a correction and a test.
\item Demonstrate how undecidable paths are adjudicated without treating absence of warnings as proof.
\item Identify every formal Required deviation and its approving authority and expiration.
\item Prove Mandatory guideline violations are not diverted into deviation/suppression status.
\item Show the scope of your release claim and the official compliance publication used to prepare it.
\end{enumerate}
